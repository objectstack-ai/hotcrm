# Sales

The Sales module covers the full **lead-to-cash** journey. The same record flows from the first inbound enquiry all the way to a signed contract, and every team member can see exactly where each deal stands.

## The big picture

A typical deal looks like this:

```
Lead  ──►  Account + Contact  ──►  Opportunity  ──►  Quote  ──►  Contract
(prospect)   (qualified)           (active deal)     (proposal)   (signed)
```

You bring leads in, qualify them, convert them into an account, work the opportunity through 7 sales stages, send a quote, and close it as a contract. Activities (calls, meetings, follow-ups) hang off each step.

## What the system does for you automatically

You don't need to do these manually — HotCRM handles them in the background:

- **Lead scoring** — every new lead gets a 0-100 score based on company size, industry, job title and engagement.
- **Hot lead alerts** — leads with a rating of 4+ stars get the next follow-up date set to today and notify the owner.
- **Probability update** — when you move an opportunity to a new stage, the win probability updates automatically (Prospecting 10% → Closed Won 100%).
- **Expected revenue** — `amount × probability` is calculated for you.
- **Big-win notifications** — when a deal of $100K or more is marked won, the deal owner is notified by email — the owner alone, not their manager.
- **Quote expiration** — quotes that pass their expiration date are auto-marked as expired every night.
- **Large deal approval** — opportunities of $100K or more are automatically routed to your sales manager for approval, and those above $500K to your sales director as well.
- **Order on acceptance** — accepting a quote closes the deal as *Closed Won* and creates its order, with the quote's lines; a contract, when the sale needs one, is drafted from the order (**Draft Contract**). An administrator can switch the automatic contract draft back on.
- **Auto-promote account** to *Customer* the moment a contract is activated, and stamp the contract's **Signed Date** if it has none. No date is stamped on the *account*: a cohort report reads the contract's **Signed Date** or **Start Date**.
- **Renewal reminder and expiration** — where an administrator has switched scheduled work on, a daily sweep gives the **contract owner** a renewal task and a reminder when a contract enters its notice window (**Renewal Notice (Days)**, 30 unless you change it), and contracts past their end date are marked *Expired*.

## Sales AI skills

The [sales skills](/docs/ai-copilot/sales-copilot) attach to the platform's assistant where your environment runs one; an agent you [connect over MCP](/docs/ai-copilot/connect-your-agent) works on the same records. Either can:

- **Qualify a lead** using the BANT method (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) and give you a score.
- **Draft outbound emails** personalised for the contact and grounded in the product catalog.
- **Forecast revenue** and flag deals that look at risk.
- **Summarise an account** before your next meeting (Customer 360).

Ask it: *"qualify this lead"*, *"draft a follow-up email"*, *"what's at risk this quarter?"*

## Where to find things

The Sales app sidebar groups everything under **Sales**, and that group carries twelve entries — nine object entries plus three that open something other than an object list:

- Leads · Accounts · Contacts · Opportunities · Contracts · Orders · Quotes · Products · Price Books
- **Sales Performance** — your daily KPIs, from the `sales_dashboard` dashboard. No sidebar item is called *Sales Dashboard*.
- **Product Catalog** — the catalogue's list prices by category, from the revenue module's `product_catalog_dashboard` dashboard. It follows **Price Books**, and leaves with the revenue module.
- **Knowledge** — for sales reps only: the published knowledge articles, so a rep finds them here and through the search box. Everyone else finds **Knowledge** under **Service**, in the Service app, which a rep's launcher does not offer.

**Products** joined this group rather than **Marketing**: the catalogue is the master data every quote line and opportunity line item points at, so it is revenue data that happened to be filed next to campaigns — see [Products](/docs/sales/products).

In the sidebar, **Contracts** and **Orders** follow **Sales Performance**, and **Quotes**, **Products**, **Price Books** and **Product Catalog** follow them: they ship in HotCRM's revenue module, and a module's entries follow the entries the app itself places in a group. A rep's **Knowledge** comes last, from the service module.

Large-deal approvals wait for you under **My Work**:

- **Inbox** — opens the platform's **approval centre**, where the approvals waiting on you are approved or rejected. **Inbox** is the label you see in the sidebar; nothing in the app is called *Approval Requests*. See [Approvals](/docs/sales/approvals).

There is no *Pipeline* entry in the sidebar. The kanban board is a tab on the **Opportunities** list page, where it sits beside the other ways of reading the same deals; the view carries its own label, **Sales Pipeline** (`pipeline_kanban`).
