**A sales rep's sidebar no longer shows the service entries.** Service Overview, Cases, My Cases and the three case reports (Cases by Status and Priority, SLA Performance, Cases Opened by Day) are hidden from anyone holding the Sales Representative position. A rep owns no case and cannot create one, and the overview read all zeros. Knowledge stays in a rep's sidebar.

The service agent, the service manager, the sales manager and the administrator see the same sidebar as before, and every persona still opens the app where it did: a rep on Sales Home.
