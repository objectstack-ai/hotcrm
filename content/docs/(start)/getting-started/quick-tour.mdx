# Quick Tour

A guided tour through the screens you'll use every day.

## 1. The home dashboard

HotCRM is six apps (section 2), and each opens on the first entry your sidebar shows unless the app names a landing for your role, so where you land depends on who you are. In **Sales**, a sales manager, an administrator or a marketing user lands on **Home**, the one pinned entry. A sales rep's sidebar has no **Home**, and **Sales** opens a rep on **My Deals**, under **My Work**: the open deals the rep owns. A service agent or service manager works in **Service**, which opens on **Service Overview**. **Home** opens the **Executive Overview** dashboard — nine tiles of revenue, customer and pipeline numbers:

| Tile | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Revenue (YTD)** | Closed-won revenue this year |
| **Active Accounts** | Customers with at least one active relationship |
| **Total Contacts** | People in the address book |
| **Open Leads** | Unconverted leads in the funnel |
| **Revenue Trend** | Closed-won revenue over the last 12 months |
| **Revenue by Industry** | YTD closed-won revenue split by customer industry |
| **Pipeline by Stage** | Open opportunity value at each sales stage, one bar per stage — value, not a count of deals |
| **New Accounts** | Account creation cadence over the last 6 months |
| **Accounts by Industry** | Total annual revenue and account count per industry |

Above them sit a date-range picker over the close date (this quarter by default), an **Owner** filter and a **Lead Source** filter. The four KPI tiles state their own period — YTD, "active", "unconverted" — and are not narrowed by the picker, so those counts stay whole while the revenue charts follow it.

For a manager or an administrator it is the daily start-of-day landing page.

Four things this page used to promise are on no tile here, and one name it used belongs to a different dashboard:

- **CRM Overview** is a real dashboard, but it is not the one **Home** opens. It sits under **Insights**, further down the sidebar, so it is a click away rather than the first thing you see. **Open Deals (Selected Period)** (a count of open opportunities), **Deals Won (Selected Period)** and **Pipeline by Owner** are its tiles, not this dashboard's.
- *Cases awaiting your response* are on neither dashboard: no tile on **Executive Overview** or on **CRM Overview** counts a case. **Open Cases**, **Critical Cases** and **Open Cases Past SLA** are on **Service Overview**, under **Service** — see [Service](/docs/service/index).
- *Top accounts by pipeline value* is not a tile in this app at all. **Accounts by Industry** groups by industry rather than ranking customers, and the nearest ranking anywhere — **Pipeline by Owner** on **CRM Overview** — ranks sales reps rather than customers.
- *Recent activity (last calls, meetings, emails)* is not here either. **Interactions Logged**, **Meetings Booked** and **Customer Minutes** are on **Sales Activity**, under **Activity**.
- *Team-level rollups for managers* are not something this dashboard does. Every tile runs the same query for everyone; what differs is which records you are allowed to read, and that comes from your permission set. Positions in this app are flat — there is no reporting line for visibility to roll up — and the **Sales Manager** set grants `viewAllRecords` outright, so a manager reads org-wide totals rather than a team slice.

## 2. The app launcher

Click the **app launcher** (top-left grid icon) to switch between apps. HotCRM ships one app per line of work, so the console opens on its home page, whose *Your apps* row lists the apps your launcher offers; click one to open it:

- **Sales** — leads, accounts, opportunities, quotes and contracts: the sales team's app.
- **Service** — cases, the knowledge base and the customers they belong to.
- **Marketing** — campaigns and the leads, contacts and accounts they reach, while the marketing module is enabled.
- **Order to Cash** — invoices, payments and the collection plan, with the contracts and accounts they belong to: finance's app, while the order-to-cash module is enabled.
- **Project Delivery** — projects, staffing, budgets, timesheets and what each project costs: the delivery team's app, while the project delivery module is enabled.
- **Expenses** — expenses, expense reports and travel requests: every employee's app, while the expense module is enabled.
- **Admin** — for system administrators.

Your launcher offers the apps your work needs. A sales rep gets **Sales**, a service agent or service manager **Service**, a marketing user **Sales** and **Marketing**, an accounts-receivable accountant **Order to Cash**, a project manager or project team member **Sales** and **Project Delivery**, and a sales manager or an administrator all of them. **Expenses** is on everyone's launcher, since everyone spends for the company. That decides what the launcher shows, not what you may open: an app left off your launcher still opens from its address, and a link to a record opens it in the app you are in.

Inside Sales, the left nav opens with one pinned item — **Home**, which is the **Executive Overview** dashboard — and everything else sits in seven groups:

| Group | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Sales** | Leads, Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities, Sales Performance, Contracts, Orders, Quotes, Products, Price Books, Product Catalog, Knowledge |
| **Collection Plan** | Billing Milestones |
| **My Work** | My Tasks, My Deals, My Leads, My Calendar, Inbox |
| **Activity** | Events, Sales Activity |
| **Insights** | CRM Overview, Forecasts |
| **Reports** | Opportunities by Stage, Won Opportunities by Owner, Pipeline Coverage by Quarter, Opportunity Funnel by Owner, Lead Engagement by Month, Accounts by Industry and Type, Customer Churn Signals |
| **Help** | User Manual, Admin Manual, Revenue Manual, Order to Cash Manual |

**Reports** and **Help** are collapsed when the app loads — **Sales**, **Collection Plan**, **My Work**, **Activity** and **Insights** open themselves. Click **Reports** open before deciding a report isn't there. **Help** opens the in-app manuals; **Admin Manual** is shown to administrators only.

The entries built on opportunities — **Home**, **Sales Performance**, **CRM Overview** and the four opportunity reports under **Reports** — are left out of the sidebar for anyone who cannot read opportunities, and for a service agent or service manager by position, should they open the Sales app from its address: and as they own no deals, **Opportunities** and **My Deals**, which were always empty for them, are not shown to them either. Every other report asks the same of its own records. **Home** is left out for a sales rep as well, which is why a rep's sidebar starts at **Sales** and the app opens a rep on **My Deals**. The **Knowledge** entry at the end of **Sales** is a rep's: it opens the published articles, and only a sales rep is shown it. Everyone else finds **Knowledge** in **Service**.

One sidebar row per destination is deliberate. Where an object offers several ways of looking at the same records, those live as tabs across the top of that object's list page, not as extra rows in the nav — so the sidebar stays short enough to scan.

Five group names this page used to list are not in the app at all, and several item names sit somewhere other than where it put them:

- *Products* is not a group. The catalog is the **Products** item under **Sales**, where the revenue master data belongs — see [Products](/docs/sales/products).
- *Approvals* is no longer a group either. Its one item, **Inbox**, is a personal queue, so it now sits under **My Work** with the rest of what is waiting on you — see [Approvals](/docs/sales/approvals).
- Four item names this page used to list have left the sidebar without leaving the product — each is now a tab on the list page it belongs to. *Pipeline* is the Sales Pipeline tab on **Opportunities**; *Calendar* and *Interaction History* are the Event Calendar and ✅ Interaction History tabs on **Events**; *All Tasks* is the first tab on the page **My Tasks** opens.
- *Activities* is not a group; the real one is **Activity**, and tasks are not in it. `crm_task` reaches the sidebar as **My Tasks**, under **My Work**.
- *Analytics* is not a group. The cross-functional dashboard, **CRM Overview**, is under **Insights**, and every report the app ships is under **Reports**, each listed above under its own name. Nothing is called *Dashboards*.
- *AI* is not a group, and no sidebar item is called *Copilot* or *Knowledge Bases*. HotCRM ships no assistant of its own; your agent connects over MCP rather than through a sidebar entry (section 8 below), and the knowledge articles are **Knowledge** under **Service**, in **Service**.
- *Knowledge Base* is not a label HotCRM carries. In **Service** the entry is **Knowledge**, and the dashboard the app opens on is **Service Overview** — see [Service](/docs/service/index).
- *Campaign Members* is not a sidebar item. Campaign membership is reached from the campaign detail page or from global search — see [Campaign Members](/docs/marketing/campaign-members).
- *Approval Requests* and *Action History* are not items either: **Inbox** is the only approvals entry the app carries. The approval audit trail is real data — the approvals plugin stores it as `sys_approval_action` — but no navigation entry in this app opens it.

Inside Service, the left nav opens on **Service Overview**, the service team's dashboard, and everything else sits in five groups:

| Group | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Service** | Cases, Knowledge |
| **Customers** | Accounts, Contacts |
| **My Work** | My Cases, Inbox, My Tasks, My Calendar |
| **Reports** | Cases by Status and Priority, SLA on Closed Cases, Cases Opened by Day, Customer Churn Signals |
| **Help** | Service Manual |

**Reports** and **Help** are collapsed when the app loads — **Service**, **Customers** and **My Work** open themselves. A user who cannot read cases is not shown **Service Overview** or the three case reports, and a sales rep who opens the app from its address is shown no case entry at all.

Inside Marketing, the left nav opens on **Campaigns**, and everything else sits in four groups:

| Group | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Audience** | Leads, Contacts, Accounts |
| **My Work** | Inbox, My Tasks |
| **Reports** | Lead Engagement by Month |
| **Help** | Marketing Manual |

**Reports** and **Help** are collapsed when the app loads — **Audience** and **My Work** open themselves. **Lead Engagement by Month** and **Customer Churn Signals** each sit in two apps; either entry opens the same report.

Inside Order to Cash, the left nav opens on **Invoices**, the first entry of its first group, and everything sits in five groups:

| Group | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Receivables** | Invoices, Payments, Billing Milestones |
| **Follow-up** | Overdue Invoices, Planned This Month |
| **Orders, Contracts and Accounts** | Contracts, Orders, Accounts |
| **My Work** | Inbox, My Tasks |
| **Help** | Order to Cash Manual |

**Help** is collapsed when the app loads — **Receivables**, **Follow-up**, **Orders, Contracts and Accounts** and **My Work** open themselves. The reps and managers who write the collection plan find **Billing Milestones** under **Collection Plan** in **Sales**, and **Order to Cash Manual** under its **Help**; either entry opens the same list or manual.

## 3. Your first lead

A **lead** is someone who's expressed interest but hasn't been qualified yet.

1. Click **Sales → Leads → New**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Company**, **Email**.
3. Save. The lead is automatically assigned to you.
4. If you have connected an agent (see [AI and Agents](/docs/ai-copilot/index)), ask it to *"qualify this lead"*: the [Lead Qualification skill](/docs/ai-copilot/skills) scores the lead 0–100 on BANT and suggests the next action.
5. When ready, click **Convert Lead** to turn the lead into an Account + Contact + Opportunity in one step.

## 4. Your first opportunity

An **opportunity** is a real deal you're working.

1. Created automatically from lead conversion, or **Sales → Opportunities → New**.
2. Set **Account**, **Amount**, **Close Date**, **Stage**.
3. As the deal progresses, move it through stages: *Prospecting → Qualification → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won*.
4. The system enforces the [state machine](/docs/administration/state-machines) — you can't skip stages randomly.
5. Win it by accepting its quote: the deal closes as *Closed Won*, a draft order is created from the quote, and the team is told. **Draft Contract** on that order drafts the contract when the sale needs one. Setting the stage to *Closed Won* by hand wins the deal without creating an order.

## 5. A service case

A **case** is a customer issue.

1. Cases arrive via email, web form, phone, chat, or portal.
2. New cases land in a queue — agents pick them up.
3. With an agent connected, ask it to *"triage this case"* — the [Case Triage skill](/docs/ai-copilot/skills#case-triage) suggests priority, category, and matching knowledge-base articles.
4. The SLA clock starts — see the time-to-respond / time-to-resolve countdown on the case page.
5. Reply to the customer; close the case when resolved.

➜ Learn more: [Cases](/docs/service/cases)

## 6. A project

A **project** delivers what was sold: one engagement for a customer, with its team, budget and cost.

1. In **Project Delivery**, open **All Projects → New**, or the **Projects** tab of an account, an opportunity, a contract or an order.
2. Give it a name and its account, and name the opportunity, contract or order it delivers.
3. Plan its budget, then send it for approval; once approved, the project manager makes it *Active*.
4. Staff it: each member is placed at a rate card, with a cost rate and a bill rate.
5. The team books hours on timesheets, and the project manager approves them from the **Inbox**. Approved hours and approved expenses make up the project's cost, against its budget.

➜ Learn more: [Project Delivery](/docs/projects)

## 7. An expense

Everyone records what they spend for the company in **Expenses**.

1. Open **My Expenses → New**, enter the amount and date, and attach the receipt.
2. Select one or more expenses and click **Submit for Reimbursement**: they go on an expense report, one report per project, sent for approval in the same click.
3. Your manager approves the report from the **Inbox** (the project manager, for a report on a project; finance too, from 2,000).
4. Finance marks the approved report paid. Nothing is paid out from HotCRM; the mark records that it was.

➜ Learn more: [Expenses](/docs/expenses)

## 8. Your agent

HotCRM ships no assistant of its own. Connect your agent over MCP, and it works on the same records you do, as you, under your permissions and sharing: it cannot read a record you cannot. Where your environment runs the platform's assistant, HotCRM's eight skills teach it to qualify a lead, draft an email, forecast, build a customer 360°, triage a case, and file and check expenses.

➜ Learn more: [AI and Agents](/docs/ai-copilot/index)
