**Sales: contracts have a Contract Name, and it is their title (#2746).** Until now a contract was titled by its number alone, so the account's Contracts tab, the contract pickers on plan steps, invoices and payments, approvals and notices showed only `CTR-0006`, and a customer's several contracts looked alike. Every contract now has a required **Contract Name**: the new-contract form asks for it first, it is the record title and the first column of All Contracts, and the account, opportunity and contact Contracts tabs show it. The **Contract Number** stays beside it — second in the list, first in the record header's strip, and under each name in a contract picker — and search finds a contract by either. The contract drafted, expired and renewal notices name the contract as `CTR-0006 · Annual Service 2027`. **Draft Contract** on an order names the contract as the order is (an order created from a quote carries the quote's name); the **Accepted Quote Drafts a Contract** flow, when switched on, uses the quote's name, or the opportunity's when the quote has none. The printed contract carries the name. The invoice and payment imports still take the contract number in the Contract column. The label is in English, Chinese, Spanish and Japanese, and the demo contracts have names.

**After upgrading, name your existing contracts.** Contracts saved before this release have no name: until they are named, their header, pickers and approvals show the record id, and notices name them by number only. They keep working, and any edit that does not touch the name saves as before. An administrator runs `pnpm exec tsx scripts/backfill-contract-names.ts --url <your org> --email <admin> --password <password>` to see what it would do, then again with `--apply`: each unnamed contract gets its source quote's name, or otherwise the account's name and its start date (for example `Acme Corporation 2026-11-24`). Rename any of them afterwards as you like. A fresh install needs nothing. The database column is not NOT NULL, on purpose: on ObjectStack 18.6.0 adding a NOT NULL column to an existing contract table fails the upgrade's schema step.
