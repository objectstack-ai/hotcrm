**The marketplace listing and the README now describe HotCRM as one business system of six apps (#2606).** The listing's description and tagline, in English and in Chinese, open with "Leads, orders, projects, cash and expenses in one open-source business system" and name the six apps as the console names them: **Sales**, **Service**, **Marketing**, **Order to Cash**, **Project Delivery** and **Expenses**. Its tags now include orders, invoices, projects, timesheets and expenses. The detail page, in English and in Chinese, follows one chain from the first lead to the cash: opportunity, quote, order, contract or project, invoice and payment, with approved hours, travel and expenses counted in a project's cost. It has a table saying who each app is for and what it does, one screenshot per app, what an agent you connect over MCP can do under each user's permissions, and how to install HotCRM or run it locally in three commands. Its links go to the public documentation at https://hotcrm.com/docs. Nothing in the app changes.
