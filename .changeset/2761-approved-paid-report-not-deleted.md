**Expenses: an approved or paid expense report can no longer be deleted, by an administrator either.** An administrator could delete any expense report, to repair one whose approval could not run. Deleting an approved or paid report released its expenses as **Approved** on no report: they stayed in the project's cost and could never be submitted again. Now deleting an approved or paid report is refused for everyone, administrators included, with a message in the user's language saying the report stays on record; the report and its expenses are kept as they were. An administrator can still delete a report that is waiting for approval or was rejected, and its expenses go back to **Draft**. The expense manual says the same.
