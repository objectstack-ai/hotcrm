# Project Delivery

Project Delivery answers the questions a delivery team asks once a deal is won: **who is working on the project, what has it cost so far, how much of the budget is left, and is it still making money?** It is a module of HotCRM with an app of its own, **Project Delivery**, where project managers, consultants and finance work.

## What is in it

| Record | What it is | Who writes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Project** | One engagement for a customer: presales, delivery or internal work | A project manager |
| **Project Member** | One person staffed on a project, with their responsibility and rate card | The project's manager |
| **Rate Card** | A grade of work with its cost rate and bill rate, per hour or per day | Finance, the PMO |
| **Timesheet** | One person's hours on one project for one period, a week for example | The person who worked them |
| **Time Entry** | One day's hours on a timesheet | The person who worked them |
| **Expense** | Money one person spent on a project: travel, lodging, meals. Expenses are their own module ([Expenses](/docs/expenses)); Project Delivery adds the project | The person who spent it |

A project hangs off an **account**, and may name the **opportunity** it was won from and the **contract** or the sales **order** it delivers. Open an account, an opportunity, a contract or an order and its **Projects** tab lists them.

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Projects and Staffing](./projects-and-staffing) | Opening a project, its kinds and statuses, staffing it at a rate card, the budget |
| [Project Budgets](./budgets) | Planning the budget in versions and lines, approving it with the project, raising it with a top-up |
| [Time and Expenses](./time-and-expenses) | Booking hours and expenses, submitting and approving them, how cost is counted |

## Where to find it

| Who | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Project managers, team members, finance controllers | The **Project Delivery** app: **All Projects**, **My Projects** and **Staffing** under **Projects**; **My Timesheets**, **My Expenses**, **Timesheets to Approve** and **Reports to Approve** under **Time and Expenses**; **Rate Cards** under **Rates**; the **Accounts**, **Opportunities** and **Contracts** the projects belong to; and the **Inbox** under **My Work**, where projects, timesheets and expenses are approved. |
| Sales managers | The Project Delivery app is on their launcher, and every project is readable, so a manager sees how the deals their team won are being delivered. |
| Sales reps, service, marketing, accounts receivable | Not on their launcher. |
| Administrators | Everything. |

A launcher decides what is offered, not what may be opened: an app left off it still opens from its address, and every record keeps the access its sharing gives.

## Who can do what

Projects are private. A person reads a project when they own it, when they are staffed on it, or when they are its project manager; the project manager also edits it. Finance and delivery leadership read every project.

| Person | Projects | Staffing | Rate cards | Time and expenses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Project team member | Read the projects they are staffed on, **without cost or margin** | Read | Read, **without the cost rate** | Book their own |
| Project manager | Open projects; edit the ones they manage | Staff the projects they manage | Read | Book their own; approve their team's |
| Finance controller | Read every project and budget; approve projects of 100,000 or more and top-ups of 10 % or more | Read | Create and edit | Read every one; approve expense reports totalling 2,000 or more |
| Project director, PMO | Both of the above; approve new projects | Both | Both | Both |
| Sales manager | Read every project | Read | — | Read every one |
| Administrator | Everything | Everything | Everything | Everything |

An administrator gives people their access with a **position** under **Setup → Position Holders**: **Project Team Member**, **Project Manager**, **Finance Controller**, **Project Director** or **PMO**.

> **Team members never see what a colleague costs.** The cost rate, the cost of each entry, a timesheet's total cost and the project's cost and margin are hidden from the Project Team Member position. They see the hours, the bill rate and the amount to bill.

> **Team members see the customer, not the deal.** The Project Team Member position reads every account, read-only, so a project's **Account** shows by name; the account's **Invoiced**, **Received**, **Unpaid** and **Uninvoiced** are hidden from them. Opportunities stay closed to them: the **Opportunity** and **Order** fields are left off their project page, the deal does not open for them, and **Opportunities** is not in their Project Delivery menu.

## The money on a project [#money]

None of a project's figures is typed in except **Planned Revenue**. Everything else moves when hours and expenses are approved, or when a budget version is:

| Field | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Approved Budget** | The total of the project's baseline [budget version](./budgets) |
| **Labor Cost** | The cost of the hours on the project's **Approved** timesheets |
| **Expense Cost** | The project's **Approved** expenses |
| **Actual Cost** | Labor Cost plus Expense Cost |
| **Budget Remaining** | Approved Budget minus Actual Cost |
| **Budget Used %** | Actual Cost as a percentage of the Approved Budget |
| **Gross Margin** | Planned Revenue minus Actual Cost |
| **Gross Margin %** | Gross Margin as a percentage of Planned Revenue |
| **Approved to Bill** | Approved billable hours at their bill rate, plus approved billable expenses |
| **Budget Status** | On Track, then Warning from 80 % of the Approved Budget and Exceeded from 100 %. Crossing into either notifies the project manager and finance |

Amounts are in your organization's currency. The project only produces **Approved to Bill**: invoicing the customer is done in [Order to Cash](/docs/order-to-cash).

## Not every customer uses it

A company that does not run projects has an administrator disable the module. Its app, its records and the Projects tabs on accounts, opportunities, contracts and orders go with it; the order page itself is unchanged.
