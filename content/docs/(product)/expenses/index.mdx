# Expenses

Expenses is for everyone who spends money for the company, not only project staff. It is a module of HotCRM with an app of its own, **Expenses**, and an administrator can switch it off.

To ask before a trip, file a [travel request](/docs/expenses/travel-requests).

## Recording an expense

Open **My Expenses** in the **Expenses** app and choose **New**:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Description** | What it was for |
| **Date** | When you spent it |
| **Category** | Travel, Lodging, Meals, Equipment or Other |
| **Amount** | What you spent, in your organization's currency. Convert a receipt in another currency before you enter it |
| **Paid By** | **Employee** (you paid and are paid back, the default) or **Company** (a company card, say) |
| **Merchant** | Where you spent it, if you like |
| **Receipt Number** | The number on the receipt, if it has one |
| **Billable** | Whether the customer is charged for it |

Attach the receipt in the **Files** tab. **Source** says how the record arrived: **Manual** when you type it, **Agent** or **Import** when an assistant or an import wrote it. With Project Delivery on, the form also has a **Project**: see [Expenses on a project](#on-a-project).

**Review Notes** is where an assistant, or you, writes what a check of the expense found: above one of the [expense standards](#expense-standards), a receipt number seen before, a missing receipt. The approver reads it in the expense list on the report. HotCRM writes nothing there itself, and it locks with the rest of the expense once the report is sent, so notes are written on each expense before it is sent. A report has **Review Notes** of its own, but a report under approval is locked as a whole, the approver included: they can be written only while the report is back in Draft.

## Submitting for reimbursement

Select your expenses in **My Expenses** and choose **Submit for Reimbursement** in the selection bar. For one expense, the same button is on its record page. There is nothing else to fill in: the expenses go on an **expense report**, and the report is sent for approval in the same click. Follow it in **My Reports**.

- **One report per project.** With Project Delivery on, each project's expenses go on a report of their own and the rest on one more report, so a trip that touched two projects makes two reports. See [Expenses on a project](#on-a-project).
- The report's **Title** is the expenses' date range. **Total** adds every expense on it; **To Pay Back** adds only the ones paid by the employee.
- Only your own expenses, on no report yet and not approved, can be sent. If any selected expense is not, nothing is sent and the message names each one.

An expense's status follows its report; you never set it yourself.

| Report status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Recalled, or reopened after a rejection. Its expenses can be changed again |
| **Submitted** | Waiting for approval. The report and its expenses are locked |
| **Approved** | Approved. Waiting for finance to pay you back |
| **Paid** | Finance has recorded that you were paid back. Final |
| **Rejected** | Sent back. Choose **Back to Draft**, correct the expenses and **Submit** again |

## Expenses of a trip [#expenses-of-a-trip]

If the expenses belong to a trip you asked for, choose **Submit for a Trip** beside **Submit for Reimbursement** and pick the [travel request](/docs/expenses/travel-requests). Only your own **Approved** requests are offered. The report is made and sent exactly as above, and names the request; a trip can be reported in several reports, and a report names one request at most. The request is chosen when you submit: it can't be added to a report afterwards. **Submit for Reimbursement** stays one click for an expense with no trip.

Such a report shows its approver the trip's estimate, what has been reported for the trip so far, and whether that is over the estimate. Over is a sign, not a block.

## Who approves it

- **Your manager**, the **Manager** on your user record. When you have none, the **Finance Controllers** decide instead.
- A report with a **Total** of **2,000** or more then goes to the **Finance Controllers** as well, and is approved only once they approve it too.

Approvers decide in their **Inbox** (under **My Work**), with **Approve** or **Reject**. **Reports to Approve** lists the reports waiting for you, to open and read first; you can open every expense on them. Nobody decides their own report, and nobody sets **Approved** or **Rejected** by hand.

**Changed your mind?** Recall the report from your Inbox while it waits. It goes back to **Draft** with its expenses; **Submit** sends it again. Only a draft report can be deleted, and its expenses stay, free to be sent again.

**Administrators** can also delete a report that is waiting for approval or was rejected, to repair. Its expenses go back to **Draft**, free to be sent again. An approved or paid report cannot be deleted, by an administrator either: it stays on record with its expenses.

**A one-person organization.** Nobody approves a report they submitted, an administrator included, so a report needs a second user who can approve it: your manager, a Finance Controller or an administrator, or for a report on a project, the project's Project Manager or an administrator. A **Total** of **2,000** or more also needs a Finance Controller or an administrator. Until that person has been added, a submitted report waits in **Submitted** and nobody can decide it: recall it from your Inbox, and submit it again once they have been added.

## Being paid back

Approved reports are listed under **Reports to Pay** for the **Finance Controllers** and **AR Accountants**. Once the money has reached the employee, they choose **Mark as Paid** and enter the date and, if there is one, the payment's reference. HotCRM records the payment; it pays nothing out and posts nothing to the books. A paid report can no longer be changed.

## Expense standards [#expense-standards]

**Expense Standards**, in the Expenses app, lists the company's standards for spending, one row per rule:

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The rule, e.g. "Hotel, tier-1 cities" |
| **Category** | The expense category it is about; empty means any |
| **Limit** and **Per** | The most that may be spent, in your organization's currency, counted per night, day, item or trip. A rule in words only, such as "second-class rail", has no limit |
| **Applies To** | Who and where, in words, e.g. "Managers and above" |
| **Details** | What a number cannot say |
| **Active** | Clear it to retire a standard without deleting it |

Everyone who has the Expenses app reads every standard. The **Finance Controllers** and **AR Accountants** keep them, and administrators can too.

A standard is advice: HotCRM never compares an expense with one, so an expense above a standard saves and is submitted like any other. An assistant working for you reads the standards, compares, and writes what it found in the expense's **Review Notes** before you send it; an assistant working for the approver tells the approver what it found. The approver decides.

## With an assistant [#with-an-assistant]

Two skills teach an assistant this module, whether it is the platform's assistant or your own agent connected over MCP (see [Skills](/docs/ai-copilot/skills#expense-filing)):

- **Expense Filing**, for the person who spent: from a receipt it records the expense with **Source** = Agent, checks for the same receipt number or the same date, amount and merchant, gives you a one-time link to attach the receipt, and writes what it found against the standards into the expense's **Review Notes**. It drafts travel requests too. It runs **Submit for Reimbursement**, **Submit for a Trip** and the other sending steps only after you say yes.
- **Expense Check**, for the approver and finance: it lists what in a report deserves a look (over a standard, a possible duplicate, a missing receipt, a trip over its estimate) and changes nothing. It offers a short text the approver may put in their own approval comment, and it never approves, rejects or marks paid.

**Mark as Paid** is not open to any assistant. The full list of what an agent may run is on [Actions Open to Agents](/docs/ai-copilot/agent-actions).

## Who sees what

You see your own expenses and reports, and the ones you are asked to approve. Finance sees every expense under **All Expenses** and every report, and so do sales managers and leadership. Nobody creates a report by hand: **Submit for Reimbursement** creates it.

## Expenses on a project [#on-a-project]

With [Project Delivery](/docs/projects) on, an expense can name a **Project**. Project staff find **My Expenses** and **Reports to Approve** in the Project Delivery app too, under **Time and Expenses**.

A project changes three things, and they are described once, with the project's time, on [Time and Expenses](/docs/projects/time-and-expenses#expenses):

- **Who approves.** **Submit for Reimbursement** puts each project's expenses on a report of their own, which goes to the project's Project Manager instead of your manager.
- **The project's cost.** An approved report's expenses count in it; marking the report paid changes nothing there.
- **The lock and the project's end.** A report on a locked, closed or cancelled project cannot be submitted: the submission is refused with the reason, and the report stays in **Draft**. **Submit for Reimbursement** and **Submit for a Trip** still send the reports of your other projects; the click ends with an error naming the report that was not sent.

One rule belongs to the report itself:

- An expense's **Project** cannot change while it is on a report, even a **Draft** one: the report goes to the approver of the project it was sent with. To move it, delete the draft report, which frees its expenses, then change the project and send it again.

With Project Delivery off, the Project field is not shown on the expense or the report, every selection makes one report, and every report goes to your manager. The project an expense or report named is kept, and shows again when Project Delivery is switched back on.

## Upgrading from per-expense approval

Before expense reports, each expense was submitted and approved on its own. An expense that was **approved** stays approved and keeps counting in its project's cost; it is on no report, so it has no payment record. A rejected one is sent like any other. An expense that was **waiting for approval** at the upgrade can no longer be decided: approving its old request answers that the approval cannot be resumed. The spender sends it with **Submit for Reimbursement**, which takes a submitted or rejected expense that is on no report and reports it like the rest, and then recalls the old request from the Inbox so it leaves the approver's list (an administrator can recall it too). Recalling alone leaves the expense **Submitted**.
