# Travel Requests

A travel request is asked before a trip: where you are going, when, why, and roughly how much it will cost. Your manager approves or rejects it before money is spent. It lives in the **Expenses** app, under **Travel Requests**.

A travel request is optional. You can record and submit an expense without one.

## Filing a request

Open **My Travel Requests** and choose **New**:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Why you are going (required) |
| **Destination** | Where you are going (required) |
| **Start Date** and **End Date** | The first and last day of the trip (required). **Days** counts both |
| **Estimated Amount** | One total for the whole trip, in your organization's currency, whoever pays for each part (required) |
| **Account** | The customer the trip is for, if any |
| **Itinerary** | The plan in your own words |
| **Notes** | Anything else |

Attach files in the **Files** tab. **Source** says how the record arrived, and **Review Notes** is where an assistant or a rule writes what it found for the approver. Neither decides anything: an assistant can draft the request, estimate the amount or check it, working as you through the API, and the decision stays with your manager.

## Itinerary lines

A trip with several legs can list them one by one under **Itinerary Lines** on the request's page. Choose **New** for each leg:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Kind** | Flight, Train, Car, Hotel or Other (required) |
| **From** and **To** | Where the leg starts and where it goes; for a hotel, **To** is where it is (**To** required) |
| **Start Date** and **End Date** | The day of the leg (required), and for a hotel the day you check out |
| **Details** | A seat, a hotel you prefer, other wishes |
| **Booking Reference** | The confirmation, once it is booked |

The legs are listed in the order of the trip. A leg has no amount: the request's **Estimated Amount** is the one total. Lines are optional, and a request with none is complete.

## Submitting and approving

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | You are still filling it in |
| **Submitted** | Sent to your manager, and locked while it waits |
| **Approved** | Yes. The plan no longer changes |
| **Rejected** | Sent back. Move it to Draft, change the plan and submit again |
| **Cancelled** | The trip was called off after approval. Final |
| **Closed** | The trip is over. Final |

Choose **Submit** to send it. **Your manager**, the **Manager** on your user record, decides in their **Inbox**; when you have none, the **Finance Controllers** decide instead. There is one step only: finance checks the money on the expenses afterwards, not on the request. **Requests to Approve** lists the requests waiting for you. Nobody decides their own request, and nobody sets **Approved** or **Rejected** by hand.

**Changed your mind while it waits?** Recall it from your Inbox. It goes back to **Draft**.

## After approval

The approval was given for the plan, so an approved request's **Purpose**, **Destination**, dates and **Estimated Amount** no longer change. Its legs are held the same way from the moment you submit: their kind, places, dates and details no longer change, and no leg can be added or removed. To change the plan, choose **Cancel Trip** and file a new request. When the trip is over, choose **Close**.

The **Booking Reference** of each leg stays open. Once a leg is booked, you, an assistant working as you through the API, your approver or whoever books writes the confirmation there, also after approval.

## Who sees what

You see your own requests and their legs, and the ones you are asked to approve. Finance sees every request under **All Travel Requests**, and so do sales managers and leadership; none of them can change a request that is not theirs. Travel requests do not appear in the Sales, Service or Marketing apps, or on the account page.

With Project Delivery on or off, a travel request works the same.
