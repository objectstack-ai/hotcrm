# Introduction

**HotCRM is an AI-native enterprise CRM.** It helps your team manage the entire customer journey — from the first marketing touch to a closed deal and beyond — with an AI Copilot built into every screen.

## What's included

The platform spans the four classic CRM pillars, plus an AI layer that knows your data:

| Pillar | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| [**Sales**](/docs/sales/index) | Manage leads, accounts, contacts, opportunities, quotes, and pipeline |
| [**Service**](/docs/service/index) | Track cases, enforce SLAs, manage a knowledge base |
| [**Marketing**](/docs/marketing/index) | Run campaigns, score engagement, measure ROI |
| [**Revenue**](/docs/revenue/index) | Maintain products, govern contracts, route discount approvals |
| [**AI Copilot**](/docs/ai-copilot/index) | An assistant that drafts emails, triages cases, summarises accounts |
| [**Analytics**](/docs/analytics/index) | Dashboards, reports, and self-service data cubes |

Everything is **wired together** out of the box — leads convert into opportunities, opportunities link to quotes and contracts, contracts trigger renewals, support cases see the customer's full history.

## Who HotCRM is for

- **Sales reps and managers** — for working leads, deals, and forecasts.
- **Service agents and managers** — for resolving customer cases on time.
- **Marketers** — for running campaigns and proving ROI.
- **Sales operations & administrators** — for configuring the system to fit your business.
- **Executives** — for the rolled-up view across the entire revenue funnel.

## What makes it different

### 1. AI-native, not AI-added

The Copilot is built into the product, not bolted on. It respects your sharing rules, draws on your real CRM data, and grounds answers in knowledge bases you control.

### 2. Process-aware out of the box

You don't have to build approval flows, SLA escalation, or campaign automation from scratch. They ship configured to industry best practice — adjust, don't build.

### 3. Built on a modern, extensible platform

HotCRM runs on [**ObjectStack**](https://github.com/objectstack-ai/framework) — a metadata-first runtime. That means every field, rule, page, and workflow is defined as code in a Git repo. No clicking through admin UIs and forgetting what changed.

If you're a developer, see [For Developers](/docs/getting-started/for-developers) for what that means in practice.

## What ships in the box

The system you get on day one already includes:

- **13 business objects** (account, lead, contact, opportunity, product, quote, contract, case, campaign, and more) with industry-standard fields, picklists, and validations.
- **A 10-role hierarchy** mapped to typical sales / service / marketing org structures.
- **Built-in automation** for lead routing, opportunity wins, contract expiration, quote expiration, campaign auto-enrollment, and SLA escalation.
- **Approval processes** for high-value discounts.
- **Standard list views, dashboards, and reports** for every role.
- **AI skills** for lead qualification, email drafting, case triage, revenue forecasting, and customer 360°.
- **Knowledge bases** for sales playbooks, product info, support articles, and competitive intel.
