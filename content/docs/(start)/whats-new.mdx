# What's New

## v6.7.0 — Latest release

> **Next release: ObjectStack 19.** `main` has moved to **ObjectStack 19.0.0-next.0** and the `^19.0.0-next.0`
> protocol range. The next HotCRM release needs an ObjectStack 19 host, and a host still on 18.x refuses it
> up front. 6.7.0, below, runs on 18.6.0.

**HotCRM 6.7.0** runs on **ObjectStack 18.6.0** across every `@objectstack/*` package, and its
manifest declares the matching `^18.6.0` protocol range, so a
runtime on a different protocol major — a 17.x host included — refuses the load up front with a
structured diagnostic instead of failing deep in a schema parse. 6.6.0 runs on ObjectStack 18.6.0
too, so a host that runs 6.6.0 needs no upgrade for 6.7.0. 6.5.0, as it was published on
2026-11-18, already required ObjectStack 18.6.0, though its notes said 18.5.0; 6.4.0 shipped on
ObjectStack 18.5.0; 6.3.0, 6.2.1 and 6.2.0 on
ObjectStack 18.4.0; 6.1.2, 6.1.1 and 6.1.0 on
ObjectStack 18.3.0 and 6.0.0 on ObjectStack
18.0.0.

**An environment on 6.6.0 reads "What 6.7.0 changes" alone.** An environment on 6.5.0 also reads
"What 6.6.0 changes", and one on 6.4.0 reads "What 6.5.0 changes" as well. 6.4.0 was the first
version published since 6.2.0; 6.2.1 and 6.3.0 were cut
in the repository but never published, so an environment still on 6.2.0 also reads "What 6.4.0
changes", "What 6.3.0 changes" and "What 6.2.1 changes" below, and their checks.

> **This page keeps the releases since 6.0.0.** The full history, every earlier
> release and the v1.0 launch included, is [`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/objectstack-ai/hotcrm/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) in the
> repository, compiled from the `.changeset/` entry every pull request adds. That file is the
> record; this page summarises the recent releases for a reader deciding whether to upgrade.

### What 6.7.0 changes

- **Invoices and payments run side by side.** A payment names its payment plan step, its order or
  its contract, or the account alone, and no longer an invoice; an invoice names the order or the
  contract it bills. **Unpaid** (amount minus Received) and **Uninvoiced** (amount minus Invoiced)
  replace the Receivable Balance on the order, the contract and the account, and **Overdue Payment
  Plans** replaces Overdue Invoices. Billing Milestone is now called **Payment Plan**, and
  confirming an order creates its plan step when it has none.
- **Contracts have a name and a form.** **Contract Name** is required and is the contract's title.
  **Contract Form** is Order Contract, Framework Contract or Other: an order contract's value
  follows its order while the contract is a draft, and a framework contract shows **Order Amount**
  and **Remaining Quota**. On an activated contract, Contract Value, End Date and Payment Terms
  change only through **Change**, which asks for a reason and the signed amendment.
- **Orders.** **Confirm Order**, **Complete Order** and **Cancel Order** are buttons on the order
  and in the Orders list. A confirmed order's amount is locked with its lines: to change it, set
  Status back to Draft, make the change and confirm again. Confirming again is refused when the new
  Total Price is below what the order has already received or invoiced. An order that takes a
  framework contract over the contract's value saves with a warning.
- **Sales.** Leads, cases and projects open on the platform's default record page, and the
  duplicate and SLA banners are gone: Duplicate Management on the lead and SLA Violated on the case
  show what they said. Sales reps open the Sales app on **My Deals**; the Sales Home and Account
  Workbench pages are removed. **Generate Quote** copies the opportunity's product lines onto the
  quote.
- **Smaller fixes** in expenses, project delivery, marketing and the manuals. `CHANGELOG.md` lists
  every change.

**Check these before and after upgrading to 6.7.0:**

- **ObjectStack 18.6.0, as 6.6.0.** A host that runs 6.6.0 needs no upgrade.
- **Two platform commands, once, after the upgrade.** Run `os migrate summary-nulls --apply`, then
  `os migrate stored-expressions --object crm_sales_order --object crm_contract --object crm_billing_milestone --object crm_account --apply`.
  The first fills Order Amount on the contracts saved before the upgrade. The second fills Unpaid,
  Uninvoiced and each plan step's Open, which otherwise stay empty on such a record until it is
  next saved. Both are platform commands that act on the database.
- **Then three scripts, as an administrator.** `scripts/backfill-contract-form.ts` gives each
  contract its form, `scripts/backfill-contract-names.ts` names the contracts that have no name,
  and `scripts/compare-unpaid-receivable.ts` prints each account's old Receivable Balance beside
  its new Unpaid and Uninvoiced. The two backfills only report until you add `--apply`, and the
  upgrade runs none of the three. `CHANGELOG.md` gives their arguments.
- **A permission set of your own that names `receivable_balance`** names `unpaid_amount` and
  `uninvoiced_amount` instead.
- **Imports.** The payment file has no Invoice column, and the invoice file no Billing Milestone
  column. A receipt split across two orders is two payment rows, each with its own ERP Reference.
- **An invoice or payment saved before the upgrade that names only the contract** of a deal that
  has an order is counted by the contract, not by the order: name the order on it.
- **Integrations.** A write to Subtotal, Discount %, Tax or Shipping & Handling of a Confirmed,
  Completed or Cancelled order is refused, and so is one to Contract Value, End Date or Payment
  Terms of an activated contract. Confirming a Draft order whose Total Price is below its Received
  or Invoiced is refused too.
- **Bookmarks** of Sales Home and Account Workbench no longer open.

### What 6.6.0 changes

6.5.0 as it was published already carried the Expenses app, Orders, Qualify Lead and the new Update
Stage. They are listed here because 6.5.0's notes left them out; an environment on 6.5.0 already has them.

- **Orders and the account's money.** An accepted quote now creates a Draft **order** with its lines,
  and **Draft Contract** on the order drafts the contract when you want one. Plan steps, invoices and
  payments belong to the **account**: the contract is optional, and they may name the order, whose
  page shows what is planned, invoiced and received. They follow the order when its contract changes.
- **Expenses, as 6.5.0 published them.** The **Expenses** app sends expenses on an **expense report**
  that the manager, or the project manager, approves and finance marks paid. **Travel requests** with
  their itinerary lines are approved before the trip, and **expense standards** guide an assistant
  that fills and checks expenses.
- **Sales.** Leads, opportunities, cases and accounts open on the platform's default record page;
  **Qualify Lead** asks for the need and the amount; **Update Stage** respects permissions and approval
  locks; activity pickers offer only the account's deals and contacts.
- **Language and analytics.** Text the app writes by itself is in the organization's language, a
  person's name reads like an address book in Chinese, and every analytics figure says what it counts.
- **Each app has its own icon**, and the marketplace listing describes the six apps. `CHANGELOG.md`
  lists every change.

**Check these before and after upgrading to 6.6.0:**

- **ObjectStack 18.6.0.** Upgrade the host first, if it is not there yet.
- **Self-hosted: one migration step.** Run `os migrate apply` once and confirm the three
  `relax_not_null` changes on the contract column of plan steps, invoices and payments; skip the
  three `tighten_not_null` changes it marks destructive. Until then a record with no contract is
  refused.
- **Hosted: expect to confirm six type changes.** On plan steps, invoices and payments the Contract
  field goes from master-detail to lookup and the Account field from lookup to master-detail, and
  the upgrade answers `409 SCHEMA_UPGRADE_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED` until you confirm them. This is
  expected from a reading on a minimal model, not from a real hosted upgrade.
- **Finish project expense report and travel request approvals before upgrading.** One still waiting
  cannot be decided afterwards (`RESUME_TARGET_LOST`): the submitter recalls it, an administrator
  sets it back to Draft, and it is submitted again.
- **The CRM User permission set no longer reads an account's Invoiced, Received and Receivable
  Balance.** A permission set of your own that reads accounts loses them unless it grants the three
  fields or View All on accounts.
- **To keep drafting a contract on acceptance**, switch the packaged flow **Accepted Quote Drafts a
  Contract** on (it ships off), and **Accepted Quote Creates an Order** off if you want no orders, in
  Studio under **HotCRM Revenue**, **Customize**, **Automations**.
- **Lead and case pages keep their warning banners**; a deal waiting for approval shows the
  platform's approval strip instead.

### What 6.5.0 changes

- **A plan for every project.** **Project Tasks** form a tree with planned dates and hours, an owner,
  milestones and predecessors, and the project page draws them on a **Gantt**. The app adds
  **Project Plan**, **Task Tree** and **My Project Tasks**. A time entry may name a task, and the
  task's Actual Hours add up. The project's **Percent Complete** is now computed from its tasks,
  weighted by planned hours. The project manager writes the plan; the team and finance read it.
- **A week of time in one place.** A time entry now has a **Project** and finds, or creates as a
  draft, the person's timesheet for that project and week. **My Week**, **My Time** and **Submit
  This Week** (with a bulk **Submit**) replace building timesheets by hand. Each project keeps its own
  timesheet and approval.
- **Budgets in versions.** **Project Budgets** carry **Budget Lines** and lock once sent. Version 1 is
  approved with the project; a top-up goes to a Project Director or the PMO, and to finance as well
  when it raises the baseline by 10 % or more. The approved version is the **Baseline**, and the
  project's **Approved Budget** is now its read-only total.
- **Order to Cash demo data**, signed copies required to activate a contract or accept a quote, an
  overpaid invoice that counts as paid, leadership read sets, localized hook refusals and many
  smaller fixes. `CHANGELOG.md` lists them all.

**Check these before and after upgrading to 6.5.0:**

- **ObjectStack 18.6.0.** 6.5.0 as it was published requires ObjectStack 18.6.0, though these notes
  were first written for 18.5.0: upgrade the host first. It also already carried the Expenses app,
  Orders, Qualify Lead and the new Update Stage, which "What 6.6.0 changes" describes.
- **Projects saved before 6.5.0 keep their typed Approved Budget** until a budget version is
  approved. `scripts/backfill-project-budget-baseline.ts` carries it into a version 1; it only
  reports until it is run with `--apply`, and the upgrade does not run it.
- **Demo data does not reach a marketplace install yet** (objectstack#34336): the Order to Cash and
  Project Delivery demo records load only on a fresh boot.

### What 6.4.0 changes

- **Project Delivery, a new module with its own app.** HotCRM now ships a sixth package,
  `app.objectstack.hotcrm.psa`, and the **Project Delivery** app. A **Project** (presales, delivery
  or internal) hangs off an account and may name its opportunity and contract. People are staffed on
  it as **Project Members** at a **Rate Card** with a cost rate and a bill rate. They book hours on
  **Timesheets** and spending as **Expenses**. Approved hours and expenses move the project's cost,
  budget used and gross margin; none of them is typed in.
- **Approvals and cost control for projects.** A project needs an Approved Budget and an approval to
  go Active: a Project Director or PMO, and finance as well from a budget of 100,000. Timesheets go to
  the project manager, and expenses from 2,000 also to finance. **Budget Status** turns Warning at
  80 % and Exceeded at 100 % of the budget, and the crossing notifies the project manager and
  finance. **Lock Time and Expenses** stops new submissions.
- **Projects Overview and the Project Delivery Manual.** The dashboard shows budget, cost, margin,
  approved hours by the week they were worked, and each person's billable share, counting what the
  viewer can read. The manual under Help explains the rules the screens do not show.
- **Who sees what in Project Delivery.** Five new positions (Project Director, PMO, Project Manager,
  Project Team Member, Finance Controller) carry three permission sets. Team members read the
  project's account but not its opportunity, and never see cost rates or margin. Sales managers read
  every project.
- **Reps keep the collection plan, and finance enters invoices and payments.** Billing Milestones,
  Invoices and Payments now ask only read access to their contract. A sales rep writes the plan of
  any contract they can read, and the Accounts Receivable position enters and imports invoices and
  payments. Nobody who cannot read a contract writes any of the three. This lifts the gap 6.3.0
  shipped with.
- **ObjectStack 18.5.0.** No HotCRM metadata changes with the platform bump itself; the Order to Cash
  writes and the weekly hours above are what it makes possible.

**Check these before and after upgrading to 6.4.0:**

- **Upgrade the host to ObjectStack 18.5.0 first**, and walk the platform's 18.5.0 upgrade checklist.
- **Give the delivery roles their positions.** Assign Project Director, PMO, Project Manager,
  Project Team Member and Finance Controller in **Setup → People & Organization → Position
  Holders**, then sign in as a project manager and see the Project Delivery app in the launcher.
- **Finance can now save invoices and payments.** The 6.3.0 note below that an administrator enters
  them no longer applies. Sign in as an Accounts Receivable member and save an invoice on a contract.
- **A tenant that does not deliver projects disables the module.** Disabling
  `app.objectstack.hotcrm.psa` removes the app and its objects with it.

### What 6.3.0 changes

- **Order to Cash: a contract's collection plan, its invoices and its payments.** Three new objects
  sit under the contract. *Billing Milestones* are the plan: what is due, when, and how much of it
  has been invoiced, received and is still open. *Invoices* and *Payments* are what finance records
  against it, by hand or imported from the ERP; importing the same file twice updates the rows
  instead of duplicating them. Reps get *Overdue Invoices* and *Planned This Month*, and see the
  plan, invoices and payments as tabs on the contract and account pages. A new *Accounts
  Receivable* position (`crm_ar_accountant`) and permission set (`ar_accountant`) carry finance's
  access.
- **Receivables totals on the contract and the account.** The contract shows *Planned*,
  *Invoiced*, *Received* and *Receivable Balance* in its Contract Value section; the account shows
  *Invoiced*, *Received* and *Receivable Balance* across its contracts in Financials. *Invoiced*
  counts issued invoices only, and the balance is what is still open on issued invoices, so an
  advance not yet set against an invoice shows in *Received* and leaves the balance alone.
- **Order to Cash is an app of its own, finance's.** The **Order to Cash** app holds the invoices,
  payments and collection plan, the two follow-up lists, the contracts and accounts they belong to,
  and the Order to Cash Manual. An Accounts Receivable member's launcher offers it alone; sales
  managers and administrators see all four apps; reps, service and marketing users do not see it.
  The Sales app keeps a **Collection Plan** group with the billing milestones reps and managers
  write. A tenant that disables the module loses the app, the objects and the totals with it.

**Check these before and after upgrading to 6.3.0:**

- **Finance cannot save invoices and payments yet.** The platform asks for edit access to the
  contract before it saves an invoice or a payment, and the Accounts Receivable position reads
  contracts without editing them. Until a platform release lifts that
  ([objectstack#33250](https://github.com/objectstack-ai/objectstack/issues/33250)), an
  administrator enters and imports invoices and payments, and a sales manager or an administrator
  builds the collection plan.
- **Totals read blank on contracts and accounts that existed before the upgrade**, until a billing
  milestone, invoice or payment is written under them. The totals are recalculated when a child row
  is written, and the platform's backfill does not run on HotCRM yet
  ([objectstack#33608](https://github.com/objectstack-ai/objectstack/issues/33608)). A blank total
  means nothing has been recorded under that record.
- **Give finance the position.** Assign **Accounts Receivable** to the people who record invoices
  and payments, then sign in as one of them and see the Order to Cash app in the launcher.

### What 6.2.1 changes

- **People keep their positions across the 6.2 rename.** 6.2.0 renamed the twelve positions with the
  `crm_` prefix without telling the platform they were the same positions, so everyone assigned to
  a position before the upgrade silently lost what it gave them. Each position now names its
  earlier name, and the first boot of 6.2.1 moves every holder of an old name to the new one, in
  every organization, as the system: nothing to run, on a hosted environment as on a self-hosted
  server. An organization that already upgraded to 6.2.0 gets its people back the same way. The
  password-based `scripts/migrate-position-holders.ts` is gone.

**Check these before and after upgrading to 6.2.1:** the move happens **at boot**. An environment
that installs the upgrade while it is running moves its position holders at its next restart, so
restart it after the upgrade (carrying holders on a live install is a platform change, tracked as
[objectstack#33238](https://github.com/objectstack-ai/objectstack/issues/33238)). Then open
**Setup → People & Organization → Position Holders** and see the people under the `crm_` names, and
sign in as a sales manager and see the accounts, deals and forecasts they saw before. A sharing
rule, queue member or record share you added in Setup under an old position name keeps that name:
re-point it to the renamed position. Coming from 6.1.2 or earlier, the checks under "What 6.2.0
changes" apply as well.

### What 6.2.0 changes

- **Three apps: Sales, Service and Marketing.** The one long menu every persona shared is split by
  line of work. **Sales** (the former *HotCRM* app) keeps leads, accounts, deals, quotes, contracts,
  activity, insights and the sales reports. **Service** opens on Service Overview and holds cases,
  knowledge, the customers and the case reports, with the Service Manual under *Help*.
  **Marketing** opens on Campaigns and holds the audience, with the Marketing Manual under *Help*;
  it comes and goes with the marketing module. Each persona's launcher offers the apps its work
  needs: a sales rep Sales, service staff Service, a marketing user Sales and Marketing, a sales
  manager or an administrator all three. On the marketplace each app is filed under the package
  that owns it, so disabling a module takes its app with it.
- **A Chinese organization gets its demo data in Chinese.** Every sample dataset ships in English
  and in Simplified Chinese, and an organization receives the one that matches its language: the
  same accounts, deals, cases and campaigns, with the same amounts and dates, so every dashboard
  reads the same in both.
- **Chinese, Japanese and Korean names read family name first.** A contact or lead whose last name
  is written in CJK script is titled 张伟, not 「伟 张」, in lists, lookups, page headers and search.
  Every other name still reads *first last*.
- **Positions carry the `crm_` prefix.** The twelve positions are renamed (`sales_rep` →
  `crm_sales_rep`), because from ObjectStack 18.4.0 a position name is shared by every app in an
  environment. Labels do not change. From 6.2.1 every position names its earlier name, and the
  platform moves the people who held it to the new name at the first boot after the upgrade. 6.2.0
  itself did not: on it, an assignment made under an old name grants nothing (see the checks below).
- **ObjectStack 18.4.0.** A record a person links to must be one they can open; the List Price by
  Category chart moves to its own **Product Catalog** dashboard; a forecast's title follows a
  rename of its owner; and Send Email goes out from the deployment's sender address with the rep
  as reply-to. `CHANGELOG.md` has each entry in full.

**Check these before and after upgrading to 6.2.0:** upgrade the host to ObjectStack 18.4.0 first,
and on PostgreSQL or MySQL run `os migrate apply` with it. Install 6.2.1 rather than 6.2.0: its
first boot moves every position holder to the `crm_` name, in every organization, with nothing to
run. The move happens at boot, so an environment that installs the upgrade while running moves
them at its next restart; until then the old assignments grant nothing. Afterwards **Setup →
People & Organization → Position Holders** lists the people under the new names. A sharing rule,
queue member or record share you added in Setup under an old position name keeps that name:
re-point it to the renamed position. Then sign in as a sales
rep and see the Sales app on the launcher, and as an administrator see all three apps. Coming from
6.1.1 or 6.0.0, the checks under the earlier sections apply as well.

### What 6.1.0 changes

The platform moves from ObjectStack 18.0.0 to 18.3.0, every `@objectstack/*` package together.
Two upgrades take a one-time backfill, and the checks at the end of this section say which.

- **Quotes price from price books.** A new **Price Books** entry under **Sales** holds named
  price lists, with one **Standard** book that follows the product catalog. A new quote starts
  on Standard, a rep can pick another active book before adding lines, and a product the book
  does not price is refused with a message naming the book. **Add Products** fills a book at a
  percentage of each product's list price.
- **Quotes and contracts print to PDF.** **Print Quote** and **Print Contract** file the PDF
  on the record's attachments. Both record the customer's **Signed Copy**, and uploading one
  fills an empty **Signed Date**.
- **New cases and leads route through platform queues.** **Support Intake** and **Lead
  Intake** still hand each arrival to the least-loaded agent or rep. When nobody holds the
  position, it waits in its queue and any member takes it with **Claim**.
- **Record titles are stored**, so an exported lookup column imports back. Accounts,
  products, quotes, campaigns and knowledge articles are titled by their name alone, and a
  forecast by its period and owner ("Q4 2026 · Nina Reyes").
- **Reps, managers, marketing users and agents upload and delete attachments** on a record
  they can edit, and delete its comments. The default **CRM User** set alone still only reads
  and downloads them.
- **Convert Lead ends on a screen that links the account, contact and opportunity it
  created**, and an account it creates skips account approval.
- **Record pages offer only the actions a record can still take.** Quotes and cases get
  buttons for their status moves, and each persona's own work comes first in the sidebar
  and in the default views.
- **In-app manuals under Help**: the User, Admin, Revenue and Marketing manuals, each with a
  Chinese version.
- **Dashboards say what they count.** KPI tiles name their period and say whether the number
  went up or down, and amounts, quantities and dates read one way across the app.
- **Large deals follow their approval.** A deal of 100,000 or more closes won only once its
  Large Deal Approval is approved, a rejected one can be sent back for approval, and only an
  administrator can create one already Closed Won.
- **More of the app reads in your language**: reports, positions, permission sets, approval
  names, the manuals' titles, the stage and priority in notices, the public forms' hints, and
  the marketplace listing in Chinese.
- **HotCRM installs as one artifact carrying four packages**: the app and its Service, Revenue
  and Marketing modules. With a module turned off, the app no longer offers the buttons that
  lead into it.

Plus a long tail of fixes — a reopened case drops its first close, Upcoming Events shows only
events still to come, Add to Campaign refuses a closed campaign and a person who opted out,
refusals name fields and stages by their labels, and docs re-measured against the app — each
with its own entry in `CHANGELOG.md`.

**Check these before and after upgrading to 6.1.0:**

- **Upgrade the host to ObjectStack 18.3.0 first.** The manifest declares `^18.3.0`.
- **Run the two backfills once, as an administrator.** Each only reports until you pass
  `--apply`, and each is safe to run again:

  ```bash
  pnpm exec tsx scripts/backfill-record-titles.ts --url https://<your-org> --email <admin> --password <pw> --apply
  pnpm exec tsx scripts/backfill-intake-queue.ts --url https://<your-org> --email <admin> --password <pw> --apply
  ```

  The first stores the title of every record saved before this release; until it runs, such a
  record shows its id in headers, lookup chips and search. The second puts each open case and
  lead that nobody owns into its queue; until it runs, agents cannot see them.
- **An import file taken before this release** that names a product, quote, campaign or
  article as "PRD-0001 - Name" no longer matches. Use the name, the number or the record id.
- **Set the organization's default currency.** On 18.3.0, amounts read as plain numbers until
  an administrator sets it; 18.3.0 asks for it on first visit.
- **Sales managers no longer delete forecasts**, and a service agent takes a waiting case with
  **Claim**: moving an unowned case to *In Progress* no longer makes it theirs.
- **Integrations.** Setting **Converted From Lead** by hand is refused with `400`. A caller that
  drives Convert Lead through the REST door or the AI tool gets the run back paused on the
  result screen, and one more resume with no values completes it. A campaign member that holds
  both a Lead and a Contact is refused on its next save until one is cleared.
- **The five retitled objects' old `display_title` column** stays in the database, unused, and
  the server logs it once at boot as an orphaned column. `os migrate apply --allow-destructive`
  drops it if you want it gone.
- **Coming from 5.x**, everything under "What 6.0.0 changes" below applies as well.

### What 6.0.0 changes

**6.0.0 is a major because upgrading to it takes action.** The database must be migrated
before ObjectStack 18.0.0 serves it, the Service Manager profile is retired, and three
campaign actions change their API shape — the checks at the end of this section say what
to do. The platform moves from 18.0.0-next.3 to 18.0.0, through 18.0.0-next.5, every
`@objectstack/*` package together. It still needs an ObjectStack 18 host.

- **An invited colleague can open the CRM without a profile.** The new permission set
  **CRM User** (`crm_user`) is the package's default: its page in the **Marketplace**
  suggests granting it to every signed-in member, and one **Grant** does that. It lets
  each member work their own leads, accounts, opportunities, tasks and events and read
  the product, campaign and knowledge catalogues; it deletes and exports nothing. On a
  self-hosted `objectstack serve`, every signed-in member holds it from the start.
- **Every report has a sidebar entry**, in a new **Reports** group, last in the list.
- **Each position names the profile it hands out**, so Setup shows who holds what. The
  Service Manager position now hands out **Service Agent**, and the Service Manager
  profile is retired.
- **Campaign enrollment follows your own access.** Enroll Members, Add to Campaign and
  Mark Responded run as the person who uses them, so Sales Rep and Sales Manager are no
  longer offered them. Enroll Members now says when it refused anyone, with both counts,
  and refuses a campaign that is not open.
- **Convert Lead names the account the lead will join** before you submit. Only Convert
  Lead marks a lead Converted, and a lead left at Converted with nothing converted can be
  edited again.
- **A contract's status follows its route.** Expired and Terminated are final; a renewal
  is a new contract.
- **A deal's line items are held while an approval locks the deal**, so its amount keeps
  matching its lines.
- **The public support form asks for the visitor's name and email**, kept on the case as
  **Supplied Name** and **Supplied Email** so the agent can reply.
- **Closing a case completes its escalation task**, and **Escalate Case** and **Close
  Case** are offered only to people who may edit the case.
- **The Pipeline by Stage tile and the Opportunity Funnel by Owner → Stage report are
  horizontal bar charts**, one bar per stage in sales order.
- **New forms no longer ask for values the app fills in**: a line's Sales Price, a quote's
  Expiration Date, and a forecast's Period End and Snapshot Date.

Plus a long tail of fixes — refusals that read the way the screens do, All Forecasts
sorted newest first, the demo accounts on the map, formatted amounts in the large-deal
alert, private deals kept out of task titles, and docs re-measured against the app —
each with its own entry in `CHANGELOG.md`.

**Check these before and after upgrading to 6.0.0:**

- **Upgrade the database before ObjectStack 18.0.0 serves it.** Every 5.x deployment
  runs on an 18.0.0 prerelease host, and 18.0.0 refuses to start on a database a
  prerelease created until `os migrate organization-ownership` has run. The steps are in
  the 18.0.0 upgrade entry of `CHANGELOG.md`.
- **Someone you gave the Service Manager profile directly** holds nothing from it after
  this release. Assign **Service Agent**, or give them the Service Manager position.
- **Granting CRM User adds to every profile.** Once it is in force, a Marketing User or a
  Service Agent can also create and edit accounts and opportunities of their own.
- **Integrations that call Add to Campaign** send the campaign as `params.campaignId`; the
  old `params.crm_campaign` is refused with `400`. Add to Campaign and Mark Responded also
  answer in a new shape; the entry in `CHANGELOG.md` gives both.
- **A front-end on another origin must be named in `OS_CORS_ORIGIN`**, and a production
  `os serve` needs `OS_AUTH_SECRET`.
- **Coming from 5.1.1 or earlier**, everything under the sections below applies as well.
