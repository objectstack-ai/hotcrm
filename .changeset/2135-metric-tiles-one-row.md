**The number tiles at the top of the dashboards no longer draw a half-empty box.** Each figure tile (Total Pipeline, Open Cases, Interactions Logged and the like) was set two rows tall, but a figure needs about one row, so the number sat in the upper half of a box with blank space underneath. All 29 figure tiles on the Sales Performance, Executive Overview, CRM Overview, Sales Activity and Customer Service dashboards are now one row tall and fit their content, and the charts and tables below them move up with them.

Nothing about the figures changed: the same values, filters and positions in each row. Two figure tiles stay tall on purpose, **Interactions on Deals** and **Open Deals** on Sales Activity, because they share their row with two charts.
