# The Collection Plan

A contract's **collection plan** is its list of **billing milestones**: each step says how much the rep expects to collect and on which day. A software deal might have an advance of 30%, 40% at go-live, 25% on acceptance and a 5% retention a year later. A subscription has one step per billing period.

The plan belongs to sales. Finance's invoices and payments report against it, but never change it.

## Building the plan [#building-the-plan]

On the contract, open the **Billing Milestones** tab and add a step for each part of the payment schedule:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Milestone** | What the step is, as the contract says it: "Advance 30%", "Go-live", "Year 2" |
| **Planned Amount** | How much is due at this step |
| **Planned Date** | When you expect to collect it |
| **Accepted Date** | For a step paid on acceptance: the day the customer signed it off. You record it; nothing fills it in for you |
| **Notes** | Anything else the team should know |

The step's **Account** is filled in from the contract when you save.

You can add, change or remove steps on any contract whose account you may edit. When a contract is amended, revise its plan to match: the plan is a forecast, never a ledger.

## What the figures count [#figures]

Each step shows three figures. You do not type them; they follow finance's records.

| Figure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Invoiced** | The amounts of the **Issued** invoices finance linked to this step. A cancelled invoice is not counted. |
| **Received** | The payments that count toward this step: those finance linked to it directly (an advance paid before its invoice), and those made against an invoice of this step. |
| **Open** | **Planned Amount** minus **Received**. It goes below zero when more was received than planned. |

A payment made against an invoice counts toward that invoice's step automatically, so finance only links the invoice. If finance later moves the invoice to a different step, payments already recorded keep the step they were given; choosing the invoice again on such a payment moves it too.

## Planned This Month [#planned-this-month]

The **Planned This Month** list under **Order to Cash → Billing Milestones** shows every step you can see whose **Planned Date** falls in the current calendar month, earliest first, with what has been invoiced and received so far. It moves with the calendar: on the first of the month it shows the new month.
