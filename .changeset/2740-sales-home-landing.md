**Sales reps now open the Sales app on My Deals; the Sales Home page and the Account Workbench page are gone (#2740).**

FROM → TO:

- **Sales rep.** FROM landing on **Sales Home**, a page of four pipeline figures, tabs of the rep's leads, open deals and open tasks, and the upcoming events. TO landing on **My Deals**, under **My Work**: the open deals the rep owns, on the standard list with its other views a tab away. The leads and tasks the page listed are the **My Leads** and **My Tasks** entries next to it, and upcoming events are the **📅 Upcoming · Soonest First** tab on **Events**. The sidebar no longer has a **Sales Home** entry.
- **Sales manager, administrator, marketing user.** Unchanged: they open the Sales app on **Home**, the **Executive Overview** dashboard. Service staff still open the Service app on **Service Overview**.
- **Account Workbench.** FROM a page reached only by its address (it had no sidebar entry). TO no such page: its rows and columns are the **All Accounts** view of **Accounts**, and its three quick filters (industry, type, owner) are on the **Enterprise Accounts** view. A bookmark of either page's address no longer opens it.

Nothing in the data or the permissions changes, and no setup is needed. The docs site and the in-app manuals say where each landing is now.
