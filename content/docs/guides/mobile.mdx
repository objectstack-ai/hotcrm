# Mobile

HotCRM works on phone and tablet through a **responsive web app** — open `your-hotcrm-url` in any modern mobile browser and you'll get a layout optimised for the device.

> Native iOS / Android apps are on the [roadmap](/docs/whats-new#roadmap).

## What works well on mobile

- 📋 **Reading records** — accounts, contacts, opportunities, cases, tasks.
- ✅ **Quick edits** — status, stage, owner, picklist fields.
- 📞 **Tap-to-call / tap-to-email** — phone numbers and email addresses are tappable.
- 📅 **Today's tasks & events** — your daily plan, optimised for the lock-screen widget.
- 🤖 **AI Copilot chat** — voice-to-text works great for drafting on the go.
- 📸 **Attaching photos** — snap and upload from the device camera (site visits, business cards).
- 🗺️ **Navigation** — addresses on accounts/contacts open in your phone's maps app.
- 🎤 **Voice notes** — long-press the activity → record → AI auto-transcribes.

## What's clunky on mobile

These work but are easier on desktop:

- Big list views with many columns (use a phone-optimised view instead).
- Drag-to-edit pipeline kanban.
- Building reports or dashboards.
- Multi-step flows / approvals (works, but tedious).
- Heavy bulk edits.

## Mobile-optimised views

Admins can ship views tagged for mobile. They typically:

- Show 3–4 columns max.
- Big tap targets.
- Status / owner / next action / due date.

Pin one for each object you use on the road (*"Today's calls"*, *"My open cases"*, *"Drop-ins this week"*).

## Offline support

HotCRM caches your most-recent records so you can **view** them offline. Edits made offline are queued and synced when you reconnect. Conflicts are flagged for review.

What does *not* work offline:

- Global search.
- AI Copilot.
- Reports & dashboards.
- New records (you can draft them; they sync as new on reconnect).

## Push notifications

Allow notifications in your browser or PWA install:

- New high-priority case assigned to you.
- Approval awaiting your sign-off.
- Lead assigned to you.
- Big-deal win in your team (configurable).
- @mentions in record comments.

## Install as a "PWA"

On iOS Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
On Android Chrome → **⋮** menu → **Install app**.

This gives you an icon on your home screen, full-screen mode (no browser chrome), and access to push notifications.

## Field-rep playbook

A typical field day:

1. ☕ **Morning** — open *Today's calls* view; review the AI Copilot's *Customer 360* summary for each.
2. 🚗 **In transit** — tap the address to navigate.
3. 🤝 **In the meeting** — open the contact; tap **Voice note** to dictate notes (auto-transcribed onto the activity).
4. 📸 Snap a photo of the venue / business card → attach to the account.
5. 📞 After leaving — tap-to-call to confirm next step.
6. ✅ Mark the visit complete; the next-step task is auto-suggested by the Copilot.

## Tips

- ✅ Install as PWA — it feels like a native app.
- ✅ Use **voice notes** — much faster than typing on the go.
- ✅ Pin 2-3 mobile-optimised views as your home screen.
- ✅ Don't try to do everything on mobile — leave reporting, dashboards, and admin work for desktop.
- ✅ Check the lock-screen widget every morning before the first meeting.
