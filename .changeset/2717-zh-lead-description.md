**The Chinese description of a lead no longer calls it 潜在客户, the account type "prospect" (#2717).** In the zh-CN pack 潜在客户 is the **Prospect** option of an account's **Type**, so defining a lead with it said that a lead was a kind of account. Two product texts did so; both now use the lead's own word, 线索, keeping the meaning of the English "Potential customers not yet qualified":

- The lead object's zh-CN description: FROM 尚未确认的潜在客户 TO 尚未确认资格的线索.
- The seeded Chinese knowledge article **HotCRM 入门**: FROM 线索：漏斗顶端、尚未确认的潜在客户 TO 线索：漏斗顶端、尚未确认资格的入站咨询, the definition the Chinese **Leads** guide gives.

The Chinese and Traditional Chinese docs give three headings an explicit English anchor, so a link to them survives translation: *Leads deliberately don't take attachments* (Files & Comments), *8. Lead routing — a queue and a rule* (Setup Checklist) and *The Lead state machine* (State Machines). The headings read the same.
