**The in-app Campaigns manual now says that converting a lead fills in the new deal's Campaign (#2632).** The ROI section said nothing fills in an opportunity's **Campaign**, "converting a lead included" (Chinese: 线索转化也不会). Since #2515 the lead conversion does fill it in: the opportunity it creates names the campaign the lead responded to most recently. The page now says so, in English and Chinese, and says when the field stays empty: when the lead was only sent a campaign and never responded, or responded only to campaigns the converting user cannot see. It matches the docs site and the Leads manual. The app's behaviour is unchanged.
