HotCRM has a new icon: a flat orange tile with a white bold **H**. The old mark, an orange gradient with two arcs and a dot, was hard to make out at the small sizes the sidebar and browser tab draw it at. The sidebar logo and the favicon both show the new mark. The app's primary theme colour changes from royal blue (`#4169E1`) to the icon's orange (`#EA580C`), so the theme and the logo match.
