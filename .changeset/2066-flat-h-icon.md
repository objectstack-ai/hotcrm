HotCRM has a new icon: a flat orange tile with a white bold **H**. The old mark, an orange gradient with two arcs and a dot, was hard to make out at small sizes, such as a browser tab. The sidebar logo and the favicon both show the new mark. The app's primary theme colour changes from royal blue (`#4169E1`) to the icon's orange (`#EA580C`), so the theme and the logo match.
