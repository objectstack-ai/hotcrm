**Lead status, the status bars, the opportunity approval fields and the conversion dialog (#2540, #2541, #2542, #2591, #2572, #2571).** The first call, meeting or email logged on a New lead now moves it to Contacted; later contacts change nothing and any other status is left alone (#2540). A new **Qualify Lead** button asks for **Need Type** and **Estimated Amount**, both required, and sets the lead to Qualified; every other path to Qualified (the edit form, the status bar, an import, REST or MCP) is refused while either is empty, by the new `qualified_needs_qualification` rule, which judges the move into Qualified only, so a lead already Qualified stays editable (#2541, ruled by the maintainer; no BANT fields). The status bars on the lead, opportunity and case pages now move the record on a click after a confirmation, and every existing refusal still applies to the click (#2542). While an opportunity's qualification or status-change approval is off (`not_required`), its edit form hides **Request Qualification Approval** / **Qualification Approval** and **Requested Status** / **Status Change Approval** (#2591). The Convert Lead dialog now says by name which contact the lead joins when a contact already has its email address, asks for a tick on a second screen when that contact's name differs from the lead's (unticked converts nothing), and fills only the contact's empty Phone, Mobile and Title (#2572, ruled by the maintainer). The suspected-duplicate note in that dialog is translated in every locale and no longer claims a second account, contact and opportunity are created (#2571). FROM → TO for an API or MCP caller: a write that sets `crm_lead.status` to `qualified` without `need_type` and `estimated_amount` was saved and is now refused with 400 `VALIDATION_FAILED`; a conversion whose matched contact has another name now pauses on a `confirm_contact` screen that takes `reuseContactConfirmed`.
