# What's New in HotCRM

Stay up to date with the latest features, improvements, and platform updates.

## February 2026 — Phase 10 Complete

### 🏢 Salesforce Feature Parity

This release closes the most critical Salesforce feature gaps, adding 25 new business objects and cross-cloud lifecycle automation.

**Highlights:**
- **Sales Cloud**: Opportunity Line Items, Contact Roles, Forecasting, Territory Management, Team Selling, Competitive Tracking
- **Revenue Cloud**: Credit Notes, Billing Schedules, Revenue Recognition (ASC 606), Payment Methods
- **Marketing Cloud**: Journey Builder, A/B Testing with statistical winner selection
- **CPQ Cloud**: Order Management, Subscriptions, Product Options
- **Service Cloud**: Chatbot Configuration, Agent Productivity Macros
- **HR Cloud**: Benefits Administration, Compensation Management
- **Cross-Cloud Lifecycle**: Opportunity→Order→Invoice, Campaign→Lead→Opportunity, Forecast→Revenue Recognition alignment
- **Salesforce Parity**: Increased from ~75% to ~92%
