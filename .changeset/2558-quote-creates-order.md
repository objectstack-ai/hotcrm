**An accepted quote creates its order; the contract is drafted from the order on demand (REQ-0010, #2558).** FROM: accepting a quote drafted a contract (a *Draft* 12-month *Subscription* at the quote's total) and created no order. TO: accepting a quote creates a *Draft* order with the quote's header and every line copied, at the quote's totals, once per quote, and drafts no contract. The order's new **Source Quote** names the quote (added by the Quotes and Products module, so it leaves with that module) and the order is listed on the quote's page. A new **Draft Contract** button on the order drafts the same contract the automatic draft drafted — same type, term and field values, at the order's total — and names it on the order; it is offered only while the order names no contract, so an order under a framework contract keeps that one, and it asks for a contact first when the order has none. A sales rep can run it, as a rep's acceptance always drafted the contract: the system writes the draft. The drafted-contract notice fires for both. Both automations are packaged flows an administrator switches in Studio — **New in Studio** on the console home page (close the *New Package* form with **Cancel**) → **HotCRM Revenue** → **Customize** → **Automations** → the flow → the switch beside *Switched on/off for this deployment*: **Accepted Quote Creates an Order** ships on, **Accepted Quote Drafts a Contract** ships off. **To restore the behaviour before this release, switch Accepted Quote Drafts a Contract on**: every accepted quote then drafts its contract exactly as before (and its order names it); switch Accepted Quote Creates an Order off as well to create no orders. The choice is kept across restarts and upgrades. No data is rewritten. The demo's accepted quote (Acme Annual Renewal 2025) is not yet linked to its seeded order (#2668).
