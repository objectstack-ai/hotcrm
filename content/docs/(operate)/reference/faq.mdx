# FAQ

## 🔒 Access & visibility

### Why can't I see a record I know exists?

Most likely a [sharing rule](/docs/administration/sharing-and-security) doesn't grant you access. Check in this order:

1. Are you the **owner**? If no…
2. Is the record **derived from a parent** you can see (a contact on your account, a line on your quote)? If no…
3. Does a **sharing rule** match the record and one of your **positions** (e.g., region, tier)? If no…
4. Has the record been **shared manually** with you? If no…
5. Does your profile have **View All**? If no…

→ You won't see it. Ask the owner to share the record, or your admin to expand the sharing rule.

Note that a shared **account** carries its contacts and nothing else: quotes, contracts, tasks, cases and opportunities on that account stay with their own owners.

### Why is a field grey / read-only?

Three possibilities:

- **Field-level security** restricts edit on your profile → ask admin.
- The record is **locked in an approval process** → wait or recall.
- A **validation rule** is preventing the change → look for an inline error message.

### I have admin access in another tenant — why not here?

Profiles and permissions are **per tenant**. You need an admin to grant you the *System Administrator* profile in this tenant.

### Why does the AI Copilot say "I can't find that record"?

The Copilot respects your sharing — if you can't see it, neither can it. Verify the record exists and check sharing.

## 📈 Records & data

### I created a lead but it's assigned to someone else — why?

Auto-assignment ran. **There is no Setup → Lead Settings screen and there are no assignment rules to check** — a lead that arrives without an owner is handed on insert to whichever holder of the `crm_sales_rep` position has the fewest open leads (`src/sales/objects/lead.hook.ts`). It load-balances; it does not match region or territory. So the owner you see is the least-loaded rep, not the nearest one.

### My opportunity won't move to Closed Won.

Three blockers, in order:

1. **Required fields** missing (Close Date, Amount, Won Reason).
2. **Approval pending** — an amount of $100K or more starts [Large Deal Approval](/docs/sales/approvals), which locks the deal until the approver decides.
3. **Validation rule** — e.g., *"Must have a primary contact role"*.

### Why did my opportunity skip back to a previous stage?

Likely a rejected approval. Check the **Approval History** section on the opportunity.

### Where do deleted records go?

To the **recycle bin**. Since ObjectStack 18.4.0, deleting a record takes it out of every list, search and report at once, and keeps a copy of it in the recycle bin for **30 days** by default; after that it is removed for good. An administrator sees each object's deleted records under **Setup → Recycle Bin** and can restore one there, or empty an object's bin, which cannot be undone. Three roles can also restore, on the objects whose every record they may change: a **Sales Manager** on leads, accounts, contacts, opportunities, quotes, orders, tasks and events; a **Finance Controller** (and the **Project Director** and **PMO**, who hold the same rights) on rate cards and expense standards; an **AR Accountant** on invoices, payments and expense standards. Nobody else can restore a record, not even one they deleted themselves: ask one of these people within those 30 days. The platform also writes an audit row for every delete, but since ObjectStack 17.6.0 the audit log shows each reader only rows about records that reader can still open — administrators included. A deleted record can no longer be opened, so its rows, the delete among them, no longer appear in **Setup → Audit Log**; only a server-side job running with system access reads them.

### My import says "0 rows imported" — why?

Common causes:

- **External ID** column not set as the matching key but conflict mode is *Update only*.
- **Picklist values** in the CSV don't match the defined values.
- **Required field** missing.
- **Owner email** doesn't match any user.

The dry-run report tells you which row failed and why. Always dry-run first.

## ⚙️ Automation

### My flow didn't fire — why?

Check in order:

1. Is the flow **Active**?
2. Does the record actually **match the start condition** at the time of save?
3. Did a **validation rule** block the save before the flow could fire?
4. Did an **earlier flow** change the record so the condition no longer matched?
5. Was the flow **re-entered for the same record while its previous run was still in flight**? The engine's re-entrancy guard skips that run and logs a warning. There is no re-evaluation count to compare against — the guard is a backstop, not a stop condition. See [Administration › Automation](/docs/administration/automation).

**Studio → Developer → Flow Runs** shows a flow's recent runs and each run's status — pick the flow you expected to fire.

### My SLA clock isn't running.

Nothing counts down in this app — there is no live SLA timer to watch. What exists is a due date stamped on every case and an hourly sweep that reports a breach after the fact. Check:

- The case is in an **open status** (not Resolved / Closed) — the hourly `case_sla_monitor` sweep filters `status: { $nin: ['resolved', 'closed'] }`, so the sweep never picks up a resolved case. A case that was already past due when it was resolved or closed is flagged on that save instead.
- The case has an **SLA Due Date** at all. Every priority gets one now, from a priority × account-tier matrix in `src/service/objects/case.hook.ts` — but a case whose priority the matrix has no row for is still left blank, and a case with a blank due date is never a candidate for the sweep, so **SLA Violated** stays `false` however long it stays open. (Typing a due date in by hand works — the sweep then treats that case like any other.)
- That due date is already **in the past**, and a sweep has run since. The sweep is hourly and only flags open cases that are already over; a case resolved or closed after its due date is flagged on that save, sweep or no sweep.

**There is no business-hours setting to check** — no working-day calendar, no holiday list, no *Setup → Business Hours* screen anywhere in this app. Every number in the matrix is wall-clock hours, and nights, weekends and holidays count against them. **There is no SLA definition screen either**: the matrix is written into the hook rather than configured, and the only part of it you set from the UI is the account's **Customer Tier**, which chooses the column. See [SLA & Escalation](/docs/service/sla-and-escalation).

### Auto-numbering jumped (e.g., from #1234 to #1267).

Normal. Sequences reserve numbers in batches for performance; gaps occur when records are deleted, validation fails after a save attempt, or batches are abandoned. The sequence is **monotonic**, not contiguous.

## 🤖 AI Copilot

### The Copilot's answer is wrong / outdated.

Three causes:

- **Stale knowledge** — there is no index to refresh: the Copilot queries published **Knowledge Article** records directly, so an edit is live the moment it is saved. Setup ships no *AI* group, no *Knowledge Bases* screen and no re-index action; if an answer is stale, correct the article itself at **Service → Knowledge**.
- **Outdated source document** — the cited article needs updating.
- **Prompt could be tighter** — for custom skills, refine the prompt; for built-in skills, file a feedback report via the thumbs-down button.

### The Copilot won't answer about a specific customer.

Two possibilities:

- **Sharing** — you can't see records for that customer.
- **There is no AI redaction rule** — no screen, setting or account flag blocks the Copilot from a customer, and nothing makes it announce one. What actually limits the Copilot is **field-level security**: it reads as the signed-in user, so a field masked on their profile never reaches it. See [Sharing & security](/docs/administration/sharing-and-security).

### Why are AI responses slow today?

**There is no AI dashboard and no AI health screen.** Setup has no *AI* group at all — that one is **Studio → AI**, and it holds Agents, Tools and Skills, not health — and nothing in Setup reports model latency. The nearest real surface is **Setup → Configuration → AI & Embedder**, which configures the provider, model and embedder and carries *Test connection* and *Test embedder* buttons: a point-in-time probe, not a monitor. Slowness is upstream almost every time — the LLM provider itself, or its rate limits. Re-indexing is never the cause, because nothing re-indexes.

## 📊 Reports & dashboards

### My report shows fewer records than I expect.

Sharing. Reports respect record visibility — you see only what you can see. A rep running the company pipeline report sees only their own opportunities.

### A dashboard tile is empty.

Check:

- The tile's **filter** — too restrictive?
- The underlying **analytics refresh** — the compiled cube is refreshed by the analytics service, on no schedule HotCRM declares, so a stale tile is a question for your deployment.
- Your **profile permissions** for the source object.

### I changed a field on a record — when will the dashboard update?

Each dashboard re-queries on the interval it declares: every **60 s** on Customer Service, every **180 s** on Sales Performance, every **300 s** on CRM Overview, Sales Activity and Executive Overview (`src/*/dashboards/`). How soon the analytics service re-compiles the cube underneath is a deployment question — this app configures no refresh schedule of its own. HotCRM's dashboard metadata declares no manual refresh control either, so any refresh button your console shows comes from the platform rather than from this app.

### Why can't I add a custom field to a report?

The field must be enabled for **reporting** in the field definition. Ask your admin to flip the *Available in Reports* toggle.

## 🔌 Integrations

### My email isn't logging.

Nothing logs it, and nothing will until an inbox connector ships. HotCRM has no Gmail or Outlook connector — `src/` carries no connector metadata and no installed platform package supplies one — so no message reaches a record because it arrived in your mailbox, whether or not it matches a contact's address, and there is no exclusion list keeping one out either. There is no `Settings` app in this product, so `Settings → Email` names nothing. What ships is the outbound half: **Send Email** on a contact writes the message as a record and leaves an entry on that contact's **Activity** timeline. If nothing is there, nobody sent one — and the action is hidden altogether on a contact with *Email Opt Out* ticked. Whether a message that was sent left the building is a different question, and it belongs to the mail transport your deployment configures. See [Email & Calendar](/docs/guides/email-and-calendar) and [Integrations](/docs/guides/integrations).

### My DocuSign envelope isn't updating the contract.

Nothing sent that envelope, and nothing is listening for one. HotCRM has no DocuSign connector — `src/` carries no connector metadata and no installed platform package supplies one — so there is no integration log to open and no webhook delivery to retry. **Setup → Integrations** is a real group, but as this app ships it holds exactly the two entries the platform contributes — *Connect an Agent* and *Datasources* — and no vendor mounts anything under it. The DocuSign row on [Integrations](/docs/guides/integrations) is design intent, not a switch. Until it ships, a quote's or a contract's status is a field someone sets by hand and the signed document is a file someone attaches — see [Contracts](/docs/sales/contracts).

### Stripe says customer X exists; HotCRM says no.

Nothing links them, and nothing will until a Stripe connector ships. HotCRM has no packaged vendor connectors — `src/` carries no connector metadata and no installed platform package supplies one — so no Stripe customer, subscription or invoice reaches an account or a contract on its own, by email match, by external ID or otherwise. The Stripe row on [Integrations](/docs/guides/integrations) is design intent, not a switch, and there is no re-link action to run. Until it ships, an account and its contracts are records someone creates in HotCRM.

## 🚀 Performance

### Pages are loading slowly.

Check:

- **Network** — is your VPN routing through a slow region?
- **List view size** — try filtering to &lt;500 rows.
- **Custom widgets** — disable temporarily to isolate.
- **Browser** — clear cache; we recommend Chrome / Edge / Safari / Firefox latest.

If persistent, check the **status page** for incidents.

### I'm hitting an API rate limit.

See [Performance & Limits](/docs/reference/performance-and-limits) for the matrix. Common causes: a tight integration loop without back-off. Add exponential back-off and respect the `Retry-After` header.

## 👥 Users & licences

### How do I disable a user?

**Setup → Users → [user] → Deactivate**. Their records are reassigned per the deactivation wizard. **Don't delete** users — it breaks history.

### Can a user have two profiles?

No. Use **permission sets** to grant extras on top of the base profile.

### Can a user belong to multiple roles?

No — one role per user. Use **public groups** for cross-cutting sharing.

## 💼 Orders and Order to Cash

### Why can't I see Orders?

Orders are shown to the people who work with quotes (sales reps, sales managers, sales leadership and administrators) and to finance, which reads them. Service and marketing staff do not see them. Look for **Orders** in the Sales app, or on an account's or a contract's page. See [Orders](/docs/sales/orders).

### I accepted a quote. Where is the order?

On the quote's page, and under **Orders**: accepting a quote creates a *Draft* order with the quote's lines and totals, and its **Source Quote** names the quote. No contract is drafted by itself any more; if the sale needs one, open the order and click **Draft Contract**. An administrator can switch the automatic contract draft back on (see [Administration › Automation](/docs/administration/automation#switching-a-packaged-flow)).

### Why can't I change an order's lines?

The order has left **Draft**. A confirmed order's lines are locked: set its **Status** back to **Draft** on the form, change them, and click **Confirm Order** again. A completed or cancelled order's lines stay as they are; its other fields, such as delivery, stay editable.

### Why can't I find the Order to Cash app?

It is finance's app, and a sales rep's launcher does not offer it. Reps find **Payment Plans** under **Collection Plan** in the Sales app, and the **Payment Plans**, **Invoices** and **Payments** tabs on every contract. If none of these is there, an administrator has switched the Order to Cash module off. See [Order to Cash](/docs/order-to-cash#where-to-find-it).

### Why can't I enter an invoice or a payment?

Only finance enters and imports them, from the **Accounts Receivable** position; sales reads them. A wrong figure is corrected at its source, in finance's own system, and arrives again the same way. See [Invoices and Payments](/docs/order-to-cash/invoices-and-payments).

### Why can't I change a contract's collection plan?

Reps, sales managers and administrators add, change and remove the payment plans of any contract they can read; finance reads the plan and never changes it. If you cannot read the contract, you cannot write its plan. What has been received and is still open on a step is never typed: it follows finance's payments. See [The Collection Plan](/docs/order-to-cash/collection-plan).

### Why didn't an advance lower the account's Unpaid?

The account's **Unpaid** adds up the Unpaid of each signed order, and of each signed contract with no order. A payment that names only the account, such as an advance, counts in **Received** only. Once finance names the order, the contract or the plan step it pays, that deal's Unpaid, and the account's, moves.

### What does Overdue Payment Plans list?

The plan steps whose **Planned Date** has passed and that still have an **Open** amount. A step leaves the list the day its payments cover it. Invoices are not followed up one by one: invoices and payments run in parallel, and a payment names no invoice.

## 🗂️ Projects, time and expenses

### Why can't I see a project?

Projects are private. You read one when you own it, when you are staffed on it, or when you are its project manager. Finance controllers, project directors, the PMO and sales managers read every project. If the Project Delivery app is missing altogether, your launcher does not offer it or an administrator has switched the module off. See [Project Delivery](/docs/projects).

### Why can't I see a project's cost, margin or budget?

You hold the **Project Team Member** position. It hides the cost rate, the cost of each entry, a timesheet's total cost, the project's cost and margin, and its budget, so team members never see what a colleague costs. They see the hours, the bill rate and the amount to bill.

### Why can't I change a project's plan?

Writing a project task needs edit on its project: the project manager, the project's owner and administrators have it. Everyone staffed on the project reads every task and reports progress to the project manager. See [The Project Plan](/docs/projects/project-plan).

### Why was my time entry refused?

The message names the reason. The usual ones:

- You are not staffed on the project: ask its project manager to add you.
- The date falls outside the timesheet's period.
- The hours are below a quarter of an hour or above 24.
- The timesheet is already submitted. Recall it from your Inbox first.
- The project is **Closed** or **Cancelled**, and takes no more time.

Submitting is also refused while **Lock Time and Expenses** is ticked on the project. See [Time and Expenses](/docs/projects/time-and-expenses).

### Why hasn't the project's cost moved?

Only **Approved** timesheets and expense reports count. Draft, submitted and rejected ones count nowhere, a travel request never adds cost, and marking an expense report paid changes nothing on the project.

### Why can't I type the Approved Budget?

It is the total of the project's baseline budget version, and it changes only when a new version, a top-up, is approved. A version's lines change only while it is **Draft** or **Rejected**, and a project has one open version at a time. See [Project Budgets](/docs/projects/budgets).

### Why can't I send a project for approval?

Its budget's version 1 goes with it, and must be ready: it needs lines and a total above zero, every labor line needs a rate card and hours, and every other line a quantity. See [Projects and Staffing](/docs/projects/projects-and-staffing).

### Where is New on expense reports?

There is none: nobody creates a report by hand. Select your expenses in **My Expenses** and choose **Submit for Reimbursement**, which makes the report and sends it in the same click. See [Expenses](/docs/expenses).

### Why was nothing sent when I submitted my expenses?

Only your own expenses, on no report yet and not approved, can be sent. If any selected expense is not, nothing is sent, and the message names each one.

### Why did my expense report come back as Draft?

It was sent on a project that is locked, closed or cancelled. Your Inbox has a notice naming the project. For a locked project, submit again once the lock is lifted; for a finished one, delete the report, move its expenses to an open project and submit them again.

### Why can't I change an expense's Project?

It is on a report, even a **Draft** one, and the report goes to the approver of the project it was sent with. Delete the draft report, which frees its expenses, then change the project and submit again.

### Why is there no Project field on my expenses?

Project Delivery is switched off. The project an expense named is kept, and shows again when the module is back on. See [Enable and Disable Modules](/docs/administration/modules).

### Why can't I see a colleague's expenses?

You see your own expenses and reports, and the ones you are asked to approve. Finance, sales managers and leadership see every one.

### Why isn't my travel request offered when I submit for a trip?

Only your own **Approved** requests are offered. The request is chosen when you submit, and cannot be added to a report afterwards. See [Travel Requests](/docs/expenses/travel-requests).
