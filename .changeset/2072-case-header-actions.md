An unowned case can now be claimed from its own record page, and a meeting can be logged or scheduled there. The case page's header offered only **Escalate Case**, **Close Case** and **Log a Call**, so an agent who opened a case from **Unassigned — triage** had to go back to the list to use **Claim Case**, and **Log a Meeting** and **Schedule a Meeting** were reachable from the list's row menu only. The header now offers all six. **Claim Case** shows only on a case that has no owner and is neither resolved nor closed, and it is the header's first button there; actions the header has no room for are under **⋯**.
