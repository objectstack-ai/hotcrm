**Project Delivery approvals and cost control (#2316, phase 1b).** A project is now sent for approval by setting In Approval, and it needs an Approved Budget to go. Every Project Director and PMO holder can decide from their Inbox, and the first decision counts. A budget of 100,000 or more then also goes to a Finance Controller. Once approved, the project manager makes the project Active. A rejected project goes back to Draft for revision, and a recall puts it back to Draft. Submitting a timesheet or an expense now asks its approver (the project manager) in the Inbox. An expense of 2,000 or more then also goes to finance. Nobody is asked to approve their own hours or spend: an administrator decides instead. People no longer set Approved or Rejected by hand. A project, timesheet or expense is locked while it waits. Each project now carries a **Budget Status** (On Track, Warning from 80 % of the Approved Budget, Exceeded from 100 %), kept from approved cost. When the status crosses into Warning or Exceeded, the project manager and every Finance Controller get one inbox notice and one email per crossing, proposing the lock. Ticking **Lock Time and Expenses** on a project blocks every submission on it, while drafts can still be written. The user docs under *Project Delivery* describe the new flow in en, zh-Hans and zh-Hant, and labels are in en, zh-CN, ja-JP and es-ES.
