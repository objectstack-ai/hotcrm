# Glossary

## A

**Account** — A company or organisation you do business with. The parent record for contacts, opportunities, contracts, cases.

**Account team** — A list of users (and their access level) attached to an account. Used when a pod works the account together.

**ACV** *(Annual Contract Value)* — The yearly value of a single contract, normalised. A 3-year $300K contract has ACV = $100K.

**Activity** — A task, event, call, or email logged against a record.

**Agent (AI)** — An AI assistant that works on HotCRM's records and actions. HotCRM ships none of its own: you connect yours over MCP, and it acts as the person it signed in as. HotCRM's eight skills attach to the platform assistant where an environment runs one. See [AI and Agents](/docs/ai-copilot/index).

**ARPU** *(Average Revenue Per User)* — Total revenue ÷ active users.

**ARR** *(Annual Recurring Revenue)* — Sum of ACV across all active subscription contracts.

**Approval process** — A multi-step sign-off flow. Locks the record while pending.

## B

**BANT** — Budget / Authority / Need / Timeline. Lead qualification framework.

**Best case** — Forecast category for opportunities likely (but not committed) to close.

**Bookings** — Closed-won contract value in a period. Includes new + expansion + renewal.

**Business hours** — The working days and hours a deadline is counted against, in CRMs that have them. **HotCRM does not have them**: no working-day calendar, no holiday list, no *Setup → Business Hours* screen, nothing in its metadata a deadline could be measured against. Its computed deadlines — every cell of the case SLA matrix, from four hours on a Critical case to seven days on an SMB Low one — are added to the wall clock, so nights, weekends and holidays all count. See [SLA & Escalation](/docs/service/sla-and-escalation).

**Budget version** — One version of a project's budget, with its budget lines. The approved version in force is the *baseline*, and its total is the project's Approved Budget; raising the budget is a new version, a top-up. See [Project Budgets](/docs/projects/budgets).

## C

**Campaign** — A marketing initiative with audience, budget, and outcomes (members enrolled / converted).

**Case** — A customer service issue. Has SLA, priority, and status.

**Churn** — Customer loss. Logo churn (# customers) vs revenue churn ($).

**Closed won / lost** — Terminal stages on an opportunity.

**Commit** — Forecast category for opportunities the rep is *committing* to close.

**Contact** — A person at an account.

**Contract** — A signed agreement. Triggers customer status; eligible for renewal.

**Coverage ratio** — Pipeline ÷ remaining quota. &gt;3× is healthy; &lt;2× is a problem.

**Cube** — A pre-modeled multi-dimensional dataset for analytics.

**Collection plan** — A contract's payment plans. It belongs to sales and is a forecast of what will be collected, never a ledger.

## D

**Dashboard** — A page of analytics widgets (KPIs, charts, lists).

**Detail page** — The single-record view (vs the list view).

## E

**External ID** — A foreign-system identifier you store on a CRM record for matching during import / sync.

**Expense** — Money one person spent for the company, recorded with its receipt. It is approved on an expense report, never on its own. See [Expenses](/docs/expenses).

**Expense report** — What **Submit for Reimbursement** makes from the expenses you select, and sends for approval in the same click; nobody creates one by hand. Your manager approves it (the project's Project Manager, for a project's expenses), and the Finance Controllers too from a total of 2,000. Finance marks it **Paid** once you have been paid back.

## F

**FLS** *(Field-Level Security)* — Per-field read/edit permissions per profile.

**Flow** — A multi-step automation, and the only rule-style automation type ObjectStack has: every *"when this record saves, do that"* rule in this app is a record-change flow. See *Workflow rule* below.

**Forecast** — Predicted bookings for a period, bucketed Closed / Commit / Best Case / Pipeline.

## G

**GDPR** — EU regulation on personal data. HotCRM supports right-to-access and right-to-erasure.

## H

**Hierarchy** — Two different things share the word, and only one of them is in this app. **Account hierarchy** is real: parent and child accounts, see [Accounts](/docs/sales/accounts). A *reporting* hierarchy that drives record visibility is not — positions are flat, nothing rolls up between them, and each rung that needs a record is named by its own sharing rule. See [Sharing & Security](/docs/administration/sharing-and-security).

## I

**ICP** *(Ideal Customer Profile)* — The kind of customer most likely to succeed with your product.

**Invoice** — The record of an invoice finance issued in its own system, against a contract. HotCRM keeps the record and what is still open on it; it does not issue, approve or post invoices. See [Invoices and Payments](/docs/order-to-cash/invoices-and-payments).

## K

**Knowledge base** *(KB)* — A library of articles. Two flavours: customer-facing support KB and AI-grounding KBs.

## L

**Lead** — A person who has expressed interest but hasn't been qualified.

**Lead source** — Where the lead came from (Web, Referral, Trade Show, etc.).

**List view** — A filtered, sorted, columned table of records.

## M

**MEDDIC** — Metrics / Economic Buyer / Decision Criteria / Decision Process / Identify Pain / Champion. Enterprise sales qualification.

**MRR** *(Monthly Recurring Revenue)* — ARR ÷ 12 (or the sum of monthly contract values).

**MQL** *(Marketing Qualified Lead)* — A lead marketing has qualified for sales handoff.

**Module** — A part of HotCRM an administrator can switch off and on: Service, products and quotes, Marketing, Order to Cash, Expenses and Project Delivery. Switching one off hides it and deletes nothing. See [Enable and Disable Modules](/docs/administration/modules).

## N

**NPS** *(Net Promoter Score)* — Customer loyalty metric (-100 to +100).

**NRR** *(Net Revenue Retention)* — Beginning ARR + expansion - churn - contraction ÷ Beginning ARR. >100% is healthy SaaS.

## O

**ObjectQL** — HotCRM's query language. `broker.find('crm_opportunity', { filters: [...] })`.

**ObjectStack** — The runtime that HotCRM is built on.

**Opportunity** *(deal)* — A qualified sales opportunity in progress.

**OWD** *(Org-Wide Default)* — The baseline sharing setting per object. Private / Public Read / Public Read-Write.

**Order** — What a customer bought this time: products or services, quantities and prices, with lines and totals. It may sit under a contract. It is entered by hand: accepting a quote does not create one. See [Orders](/docs/sales/orders).

## P

**Pipeline** — All open opportunities, regardless of stage.

**Permission set** — A bundle of extra permissions granted on top of a user's profile.

**Position** — A flat group used to hand out record access (`crm_sales_manager`, `crm_eu_sales_team`, …). Positions do not nest and nothing rolls up between them: a record reaches a position only through a sharing rule that names it, so a position no rule names grants nothing. A user may hold several.

**Profile** — The base permissions assigned to every user.

**PWA** *(Progressive Web App)* — Install HotCRM as an "app" on your phone home screen.

**Payment** — The record of money received against a contract, an invoice or a payment plan. Finance writes it; sales never does.

**Payment plan** — One step of a contract's [collection plan](/docs/order-to-cash/collection-plan): how much sales expects to collect, and on which day. What has been invoiced, received and is still open on it follows finance's invoices and payments. Part of the Order to Cash module.

**Project** — One engagement for a customer, under an account: presales, delivery or internal work. Its cost and margin fill themselves from approved timesheets and expenses. See [Project Delivery](/docs/projects).

**Project member** — One person staffed on a project, at a rate card. Only the project's members book hours on it.

**Project task** — A step of a project's plan, drawn on the Gantt. The project's Percent Complete follows its tasks. Not the same as a *Task*. See [The Project Plan](/docs/projects/project-plan).

## Q

**Qualification** — Determining if a lead/opportunity is worth pursuing.

**Quote** — A formal price proposal sent to a customer.

**Quota** — A rep's or team's revenue target for a period.

## R

**RAG** *(Retrieval-Augmented Generation)* — Retrieving the relevant knowledge, such as published knowledge articles, before generating an answer, so the answer rests on your own content.

**Renewal** — Extending a contract before / at its end date.

**Role** — The v1 name for a **position**, kept here because older screens and imports still use it. The thing itself is a position; see *Position*.

**Rate card** — A grade of work with its cost rate and bill rate, per hour or per day. Project team members do not see the cost rate.

## S

**SCIM** — Standard for syncing users from your identity provider (Okta, Azure AD).

**SDR / BDR** *(Sales Development Rep / Business Development Rep)* — A rep specialised in prospecting leads.

**Sharing rule** — An exception that widens record access beyond the OWD baseline, to the positions it names.

**Skill (AI)** — A bite-sized AI capability (qualify lead, draft email, triage case).

**SLA** *(Service Level Agreement)* — A commitment to first-respond / resolve a case within X time.

**SOC2** — A security/audit standard for service providers.

**SQL** — We use ObjectQL instead. Don't write SQL.

**SSO** *(Single Sign-On)* — Log in to HotCRM with your corporate IdP (Okta, Azure AD, Google).

**Stage** — A step in the opportunity lifecycle.

**State machine** — Defines valid status transitions and what triggers each.

**`sys_activity`** — Platform-provided object used to record any time-stamped activity on a record (call, meeting, task completion). The "Log a Call" action writes here, and entries surface in a record's Activity tab.

**`sys_audit_log`** — Platform field-history store. Any field on an object declared with `trackHistory: true` (the default for every CRM object) generates a `sys_audit_log` row on change. Visible in the Activity tab and in admin-facing audit reports.

**`sys_comment`** — Platform-provided threaded discussion / chatter store. Comments posted on any record (case, opportunity, account…) live here. The Activity tab pulls them in along with `sys_activity`, `sys_email` and audit history.

**`sys_email`** — Platform-provided outbound email queue. The "Send Email" action enqueues here; the email-service worker handles delivery, retries and status (queued / sent / bounced / failed).

## T

**Tenant** — A logically isolated HotCRM instance (one customer org).

**TCV** *(Total Contract Value)* — The full multi-year value of a contract (vs ACV which normalises to one year).

**Task** — A to-do on a record: a call to make, a document to send. It belongs to the core and is listed under **My Tasks**. A step of a project's plan is a *Project task*.

**Time entry** — One day's hours on a timesheet, priced at the rates of the person's staffing.

**Timesheet** — One person's hours on one project for one period, usually a week. The project's Project Manager approves it, and only approved hours count in the project's cost. See [Time and Expenses](/docs/projects/time-and-expenses).

**Travel request** — A trip asked for before it happens: where, when, why and roughly how much. The traveler's manager approves it, or the project's Project Manager when it is for a project. It is a plan, not a cost. See [Travel Requests](/docs/expenses/travel-requests).

## U

**User** — A person who can log in. Each has a profile, and any number of positions.

## V

**View** — Saved list-view filters/columns/sort.

**View All** — A profile permission that bypasses sharing rules for an object.

## W

**Webhook** — An HTTP callback fired when CRM events happen.

**Workflow rule** — Not an ObjectStack concept. The platform retired the standalone workflow-rule type (ADR-0019 / ADR-0020): there is no `workflow` metadata type, no `workflows` collection on a stack, and no *Workflow Rules* entry under **Studio → Automation** — only *Flows*. A simple after-save rule (condition → field update / email / task) is authored as a **record-change flow**; see [Administration › Automation](/docs/administration/automation).

**Win rate** — Closed-won ÷ (closed-won + closed-lost). Excludes "closed without decision".

## Y

**YoY** *(Year-over-Year)* — This year vs last year, same period.
