# The Collection Plan

A deal's **collection plan** is its list of **payment plans**: each step says how much the rep expects to collect and on which day. A plan is made on an **order**, or on a **contract** when the deal has no order. The steps belong to the account, so the account's page lists the plans of all its orders and contracts. A software deal might have an advance of 30%, 40% at go-live, 25% on acceptance and a 5% retention a year later. A subscription has one step per billing period.

The plan belongs to sales. Finance's payments report against it, but never change it; invoices run beside it and name the order or the contract.

## Building the plan [#building-the-plan]

On the order, open the **Payment Plans** tab and add a step for each part of the payment schedule; for a deal with no order, do the same on the contract. A step always names its order or its contract: one with neither is refused.

**One deal's plan hangs in one place: on the order when there is one, and on the contract only when there is no order.** Nothing stops a second plan for the same deal on the other record, so keep to one: the two pages would each show a plan.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Milestone** | What the step is, as the contract says it: "Advance 30%", "Go-live", "Year 2" |
| **Planned Amount** | How much is due at this step |
| **Planned Date** | When you expect to collect it |
| **Accepted Date** | For a step paid on acceptance: the day the customer signed it off. You record it; nothing fills it in for you |
| **Notes** | Anything else the team should know |

Choosing the **Order** or the **Contract** fills in the step's **Account**. An order or a contract of another account is refused, naming it. When the order sits under a contract (a framework or subscription contract), the step takes that contract too, so the order's page shows its own plan and the contract's page totals the plans of all its orders, each step counted once. A step that names the order and a different contract is refused, and so is an order and a contract of two different accounts.

Reps, sales managers and administrators add, change and remove the steps of any account they can read; editing the account or the contract is not needed. Finance reads the plan and never changes it. When a contract is amended, revise its plan to match: the plan is a forecast, never a ledger.

## The step a confirmed order gets [#confirmed-order]

When an order becomes **Confirmed** and has no payment plan yet, HotCRM adds one step: the order's **Total Price**, planned for today plus the order's **Payment Terms** (Net 15, 30, 60 or 90 days; Due on Receipt, or no terms, today). An order that already has a plan gets none, and confirming it again adds none. Instalments stay yours: change the step's amount and add the others.

When the order's total changes later, a step that is the order's only one and has received nothing follows the new total. Otherwise the plan stays as it is, and the order shows **Payment Plan Differs** while it has steps that add up to anything other than its total: change, split or add steps until they match.

## What the figures count [#figures]

Each step shows two figures. You do not type them; they follow finance's payments.

| Figure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Received** | The payments that name this step. |
| **Open** | **Planned Amount** minus **Received**. It goes below zero when more was received than planned. |

**Open** is stored on the step, so any list of payment plans can be filtered and sorted on it.

## Planned This Month [#planned-this-month]

The **Planned This Month** list, under **Follow-up → Planned This Month** in the **Order to Cash** app and as a tab of the **Payment Plans** list (**Collection Plan → Payment Plans** in **Sales**), shows every step you can see whose **Planned Date** falls in the current calendar month, earliest first, with what has been received so far. It moves with the calendar: on the first of the month it shows the new month.

## Overdue Payment Plans [#overdue]

The **Overdue Payment Plans** list, under **Follow-up → Overdue Payment Plans** in the **Order to Cash** app and as a tab of the **Payment Plans** list, shows every step you can see whose **Planned Date** is before today and that still has an **Open** amount, oldest first. A step leaves the list the day its payments cover it.
