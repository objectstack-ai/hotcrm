**The Sales Home page is now headed Sales Home, the name its sidebar entry gives it.** The page used to head itself **Sales Dashboard**, so a rep clicked **Sales Home** and landed on a page with a different name. That name also belongs to a different place: the sales dashboard opens from **Sales Performance**. The heading now matches the sidebar entry in every language the app ships: 销售主页 in Chinese, Inicio de Ventas in Spanish and 営業ホーム in Japanese.

The **Welcome back** line under the heading is gone. It greeted no one by name and said nothing about the page. The heading is now followed by one line, the page's description, which says what the page holds: key metrics, your open work and upcoming events.
