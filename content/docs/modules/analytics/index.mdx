# Analytics & Business Intelligence

Data without insight is just noise. HotCRM includes a powerful, embedded analytics engine that understands your metadata and permissions structure natively — no separate BI tool required. Every cloud comes with pre-built dashboards, and natural language queries put the answers to any business question at your fingertips.

## Operational Reports

### Real-Time Reporting

Reports run on live data — no nightly syncs, no stale dashboards.

- **Drag-and-Drop Builder**: Business users can create reports without IT help — group, filter, summarize, and sort with clicks
- **Cross-Object Reporting**: Join data across clouds (e.g., "Show me Accounts with open Opportunities AND overdue Cases")
- **Report Types**: Tabular, Summary, Matrix, and Joined reports for every analytical need
- **Scheduling**: Automate report delivery via email on daily, weekly, or monthly schedules
- **Export**: One-click export to Excel, CSV, or PDF for offline analysis and presentations

### Interactive Dashboards

Visual dashboards that tell the story behind your numbers.

- **Visualization Types**: Charts, Gauges, Funnels, Heatmaps, KPI Cards, and Trend Lines
- **Dynamic Filters**: Slice any dashboard by Region, Time Period, Team, Product, or custom dimensions
- **Drill-Down**: Click on any chart segment to see the underlying records that make up the number
- **Role-Based Dashboards**: Executives, managers, and individual contributors each see the metrics most relevant to their role
- **Mobile Optimized**: Dashboards render responsively on tablets and phones for on-the-go access

## Sales Analytics

Track every aspect of your sales engine.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pipeline Value** | Total value of all open opportunities by stage |
| **Win Rate** | Percentage of opportunities that close as "Won" |
| **Average Deal Size** | Mean opportunity amount across the pipeline |
| **Sales Cycle Length** | Average days from opportunity creation to close |
| **Lead Conversion Rate** | Percentage of leads successfully converted to opportunities |
| **Quota Attainment** | Individual and team progress against revenue targets |
| **Forecast Accuracy** | Predicted vs. actual closed revenue for each period |
| **Activity Metrics** | Calls, emails, and meetings per rep per week |

## Marketing Analytics

Measure the effectiveness of every marketing dollar.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Campaign ROI** | Revenue generated divided by campaign cost |
| **Cost Per Lead (CPL)** | Total marketing spend divided by leads captured |
| **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)** | Total cost to acquire one paying customer |
| **Email Open Rate** | Percentage of delivered emails that are opened |
| **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** | Percentage of opens that result in a link click |
| **MQL to SQL Conversion** | Rate at which marketing-qualified leads become sales-qualified |
| **Channel Performance** | Side-by-side comparison of ROI by marketing channel |
| **Funnel Velocity** | Speed at which leads progress through the marketing funnel |

## Service Analytics

Monitor support quality and customer satisfaction.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Average Response Time** | Mean time to first agent response |
| **Average Resolution Time** | Mean time from case open to close |
| **SLA Compliance Rate** | Percentage of cases meeting SLA targets |
| **First Contact Resolution (FCR)** | Cases resolved on the first interaction |
| **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)** | Post-case satisfaction survey scores |
| **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** | Customer loyalty and advocacy metric |
| **Case Backlog** | Open cases by age, priority, and assigned agent |
| **Agent Utilization** | Cases per agent with capacity and workload tracking |

## Revenue Analytics

Track the financial health of your business.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total Contract Value (TCV)** | Sum of all active contract amounts |
| **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)** | Subscription revenue normalized to an annual figure |
| **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** | Subscription revenue on a monthly basis |
| **Quote-to-Close Ratio** | Percentage of quotes that convert to signed contracts |
| **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)** | Average number of days to collect payment |
| **Renewal Rate** | Percentage of contracts that renew at expiration |
| **Average Contract Length** | Mean duration of active contracts in months |
| **Invoice Aging** | Outstanding invoices grouped by 30/60/90+ day buckets |

## HR Analytics

Measure workforce health and talent effectiveness.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Time-to-Hire** | Average days from requisition opening to offer acceptance |
| **Cost-per-Hire** | Total recruiting spend divided by number of hires |
| **Employee Turnover** | Annualized rate of voluntary and involuntary departures |
| **Offer Acceptance Rate** | Percentage of extended offers that are accepted |
| **Average Performance Rating** | Mean review score across the organization |
| **Training Completion Rate** | Percentage of assigned training courses completed |
| **Leave Utilization** | Percentage of available PTO days actually used |
| **Headcount vs. Budget** | Actual FTE compared to budgeted positions |

## AI-Powered Insights

Go beyond static reports with intelligent, proactive analysis.

- **Trend Detection**: "Pipeline is trending down 15% compared to last quarter — here are the contributing factors"
- **Anomaly Alerts**: "Unusual spike in Case Volume detected in the Europe region this week"
- **Predictive Forecasting**: AI-adjusted revenue forecasts that account for deal velocity, engagement signals, and historical patterns
- **Natural Language Queries**: Ask questions in plain English — "Show me all deals in California over $50K that haven't been updated in 10 days"
- **Explainable AI**: Click "Why?" on any metric to see the leading factors behind the number (e.g., "Win Rate dropped because Competitor X increased their discounting")
- **Automated Recommendations**: "Leads from Webinar campaigns convert 3x better than Email campaigns — consider reallocating budget"

## Data Integration

For organizations that need to combine CRM data with external sources.

- **Data Warehouse Sync**: Native connectors to push data to Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift for enterprise-scale analytics
- **External Objects**: View data from ERP and legacy systems inside HotCRM without copying it (data virtualization)
- **API Access**: RESTful and GraphQL APIs for building custom analytics dashboards and data pipelines
- **Scheduled Exports**: Automated data extracts on a schedule for regulatory reporting or downstream systems

## Best Practices

1. **Start with Questions**: Define the business questions you need answered before building dashboards
2. **Less is More**: A dashboard with 5 well-chosen metrics beats one with 50 that nobody reads
3. **Automate Distribution**: Schedule key reports to arrive in stakeholders' inboxes every Monday morning
4. **Act on Insights**: Analytics only matter if they drive action — every dashboard should lead to a decision
5. **Trust AI Recommendations**: The system sees patterns across thousands of records that humans miss — let it guide your strategy
