# Price Books

A **price book** is a named list of product prices. Every product has one **List Price** on the catalog; a price book lets the same product carry a different price for a different audience — a partner tier, a region, or a promotion that runs between two dates.

A price book holds one **price book entry** per product: the product, its **List Price** in that book, and whether the entry is **Active**.

## The standard price book

Exactly one price book is the **Standard** book, and HotCRM keeps it single:

- A second book cannot be marked **Standard**. The save is refused and names the book that already is.
- **Standard** cannot be turned off on the standard book, and the standard book cannot be deleted. To stop using it, turn off **Active**.

HotCRM's sample data includes a book named **Standard** with one entry for every product in the sample catalog, at that product's **List Price**. An organization whose sample data is in Simplified Chinese gets the same catalog with Chinese product names and descriptions instead, at the same prices, and its book is named **标准价格表**. A single-organization installation that already has data gets the same book once, on its first start after the upgrade; after that, prices you change in it stay changed.

After that, the Standard book follows the catalog. A product added later gets its Standard entry when it is saved, at its **List Price**, and changing a product's **List Price** reprices its Standard entry. The product's **List Price** is the standard price, with its rule that the price is not below **Cost**: change the standard price on the product.

## One entry per product

A price book prices each product once. Adding a second entry for a product the book already prices is refused, and the message names the book and the product, so you know which entry to edit instead. The same product can of course have an entry in every book.

## Validity

**Valid From** and **Valid To** record the window a book is meant for — a promotion's start and end. Either may be left empty for an open-ended book, and **Valid To** may not fall before **Valid From**.

## Quoting from a price book

Every quote prices from one price book, shown as **Price Book** on the quote.

- **A new quote starts on the Standard book.** Leave **Price Book** empty and the quote is saved with the Standard book. To quote from another book, pick it on the quote before adding line items. Only an active book can be picked.
- **A line takes its list price from the book.** When you pick a product on a quote line, its **List Price** is that product's price in the quote's book, and on a new line **Sales Price** starts at that price too. Negotiate from there as before.
- **A product the book does not price is refused.** If the quote's book has no active entry for the product, the line is not saved, and the message names the book and the product. Add the product to the book, or quote from another book.
- **Changing the book.** A quote's book can change while the quote has no line items. Once it has some, they were priced from the old book, so the change is refused: remove the line items, choose the book, and add them again.
- **Prices already quoted stay.** Repricing an entry, or editing the book, changes only lines written afterwards. A line keeps the price it was given, as it does when a product's **List Price** changes.
- **Settled quotes keep their book.** Once a quote is accepted, rejected or expired it is frozen, its **Price Book** with it.

A book that any quote prices from cannot be deleted; the message says how many quotes use it. To stop quoting from it, turn off **Active**: it leaves the quote's picker, and the quotes already on it keep it.

Deals do not read price books. An opportunity's line items still take their list price from the product, that is, from the standard price.

A quote that was created before price books priced quotes has no **Price Book** of its own. Its new line items price from the Standard book.

## Adding products to a book

On a price book's record page, **Add Products** prices the catalog in that book in one step. You enter one figure, **Price as % of List Price**: 80 for a partner tier 20% below the standard price, 110 for a region priced above it. Every active product the book does not price yet gets an entry at that share of its **List Price**, rounded to the cent. Products the book already prices keep their price, so running it again after new products arrive only tops the book up. The result screen says how many products were added and how many were already priced.

To set one product's price by hand, open the book's **Price Book Entries** tab and edit that entry, or add a new one there. A product's record page lists its entries in every book under **Price Book Entries**.

## Who can change price books

| Role | Price books and their entries |
| --- | --- |
| **System administrator** | Create, edit and delete any book and any entry. |
| **Sales manager** | Create books, and edit the books they own: add, reprice and remove their entries, and run **Add Products** on them. |
| **Sales rep** | Read every book and every entry, and choose a book on their quotes. |

Every price book is visible to everyone who can read it — the same sharing as the product catalog — and an entry is visible and editable to whoever can see and edit its book. **Add Products** adds each entry as the person running it: on a book they may not edit, every product is refused, and the run ends with the counts.

## Currency

A price book has no currency of its own. Every price in every book is in your organization's default currency, as every amount in HotCRM is. Multi-currency pricing waits for the ObjectStack platform to support per-record currency and exchange rates.
