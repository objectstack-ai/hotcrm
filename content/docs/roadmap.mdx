# HotCRM Roadmap

HotCRM has completed **13 development phases** (including 10.5, 10.6, and 12A-D), establishing a full-featured, production-ready **13-package CRM** with **~148 business objects**, AI agent workflows, enterprise security, full UI metadata, Salesforce feature parity (~95%), 4 vertical industry solutions, and comprehensive developer tooling. Our roadmap now focuses on **module optimization**, **seed data foundation**, and **advanced AI** for 2027+.

> **Protocol Version**: @objectstack/spec v3.0.0 | **Tests**: 3,318+ passing (~173+ files) | **Spec Schema Adoption**: ~65 / ~80 (81%) | **Last Updated**: February 16, 2026
