# Orders

An **order** records what a customer bought this time: the products or services, how many, and at what price. It is the record a deal ends in. A **contract** records what the two sides agreed, and is optional: an order can sit under a framework contract, and most orders have none.

| | Order | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Records | What was bought this time | What the two sides agreed |
| Lines | Yes; the subtotal is the sum of the lines | No lines |
| Dates | Order date, shipped date | Start, end, term, renewal |
| When | A sale | A subscription, project services, a framework agreement, an NDA |

An order belongs to at most one contract; a contract may hold many orders, or none.

That contract is always one of the order's own account. The order form offers only the account's contracts under **Contract**, and clears **Contract** when you change the account. A save that names another account's contract anyway (through the API, for instance) is refused, with the message on **Contract**: choose one of the account's contracts, or clear **Contract**. An order saved with another account's contract before this rule existed is refused on its next edit until its contract or its account is corrected.

## Where to find orders

- **Orders** in the **Sales** group of the Sales app.
- The **Orders** list on an account's page, and on the page of a contract an order sits under.
- **Orders, Contracts and Accounts** in the Order to Cash app, where finance reads them.

Orders are shown to the people who work with quotes: sales reps, sales managers, sales leadership and administrators, plus finance (Accounts Receivable and the Finance Controller), who read them. Service and marketing staff do not see them.

## An accepted quote creates its order

When a quote is **Accepted**, its order appears by itself: a **Draft** order with the quote's name, account, contact, opportunity and owner, its discount, tax, shipping and payment terms, its addresses and description, and every quote line copied one to one. The totals are the quote's. The order's **Source Quote** names the quote, and the order is listed on the quote's page. Each accepted quote gets one order, however often its status is saved.

Nothing else is created: accepting a quote no longer drafts a contract. If the sale needs one, draft it from the order (below).

**Source Quote** is filled in by the system and cannot be changed by hand. It comes with the Quotes and Products module: where an administrator has switched that module off, quotes, and so this field, are not there, and orders are entered by hand.

## Drafting the contract from an order

A subscription or a project usually needs a contract; a product sale usually does not. **Draft Contract** on the order's page drafts it: a **Draft** contract typed *Subscription*, on a 12-month term starting today, at the order's total, named as the order is (an order created from a quote carries the quote's name), with the order's account, contact, opportunity, owner and payment terms. It is the same starting draft an accepted quote used to create; open it to complete the term, the dates and the type. The order then names the contract under **Contract**, and you get a **Contract drafted** notice with a link.

- **Draft Contract** is offered only while the order names no contract. An order placed under an existing framework contract names that contract in **Contract**, and is not offered a second one.
- A **Cancelled** order is not offered **Draft Contract**, and nothing is drafted for one.
- The order needs a **Contact**: a contract names its primary contact. Without one, nothing is drafted and you are told to add the contact first.
- A sales rep may run it, though a rep cannot create a contract by hand: the system writes the draft.

## Before this release: every accepted quote drafted a contract

Until this release, accepting a quote drafted a contract and created no order. Now it creates the order, and the contract is drafted from the order when the sale needs one. Both automations are flows an administrator can switch, in Studio (**New in Studio** on the console home page → **HotCRM Revenue** → **Customize** → **Automations**; the steps are in [Administration › Automation](/docs/administration/automation#switching-a-packaged-flow)):

| Flow | Ships | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Accepted Quote Creates an Order** | On | Creates the draft order described above |
| **Accepted Quote Drafts a Contract** | Off | Drafts a contract for every accepted quote, as before this release |

To keep the old behaviour, switch **Accepted Quote Drafts a Contract** on. Every accepted quote then drafts its contract exactly as it used to, and the quote's order names that contract. Switch **Accepted Quote Creates an Order** off as well if you want no orders at all. The choice is kept across restarts and upgrades. See [Administration › Automation](/docs/administration/automation#switching-a-packaged-flow).

## The statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| ⚪ **Draft** | Being put together *(default for new orders)* |
| 🔵 **Confirmed** | Agreed with the customer; its lines and amount are locked |
| 🟢 **Completed** | Delivered and done |
| ⚫ **Cancelled** | Will not go ahead |

An order moves **Draft → Confirmed → Completed**. **Confirmed** can go back to **Draft**, and **Draft** or **Confirmed** can be **Cancelled**. **Completed** and **Cancelled** are final; any other status change is refused, with a message that names the allowed moves.

Move an order with the buttons on its page, also in the row menu of the Orders list: **Confirm Order** on a *Draft* order, **Complete Order** on a *Confirmed* one, and **Cancel Order** on either. Only the moves the order can make from its status are offered. Each button asks first and says what the move does: confirming locks the lines, and completing or cancelling is final. A move that is no longer allowed by the time you click (someone cancelled the order a moment ago) is refused with that message, and nothing changes.

**Status** on the order's form still changes the status for now, held to the same arrows. A *Confirmed* order goes back to *Draft* that way: that move has no button.

## What an order record stores

| Section | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Order Information** | Order number, name, owner, account, contact, opportunity, status, order date, contract, customer PO number, description |
| **Pricing** | Subtotal, discount %, discount amount, tax, shipping & handling, total price, payment terms |
| **Delivery** | Fulfillment status, shipped date |
| **Addresses** | Billing address, shipping address |
| **System** | External reference |

The **Customer PO Number** is the number on the customer's own purchase order. The **External Reference** is the order's number in another system, such as the order system it came from; no two orders share one.

## Order lines

Each line carries a product, its title, a description, the quantity, the list price, the sales price, a line discount, and the line's subtotal and total, the same as a quote line.

- **Choose a product** and the line takes the product's name as its title and the product's list price, and the sales price starts at the list price until you type another.
- **No product?** Type the line's **Product Title** and **Sales Price**. This is how you add something the catalogue does not sell, and how lines are entered when the Quotes and Products module is switched off (its product picker is then empty).
- A line's list price comes from the product itself, not from a price book.

## How the totals are calculated

```
Subtotal         = Σ line (quantity × sales price × (1 − line discount %))
Discount Amount  = Subtotal × order discount %
Total Price      = Subtotal − Discount Amount + Tax + Shipping & Handling
```

The subtotal is recalculated on every line you add, change or remove, and the discount amount and total price follow every change to the subtotal, the discount, the tax or the shipping. On an order with lines the subtotal cannot be typed: a save that changes it to anything other than the sum of the lines is refused, and you change the lines instead. An order with no lines keeps the subtotal you type.

## A confirmed order's lines and amount are locked

Once an order leaves **Draft**, its lines cannot be added, changed or removed, and the **Order Lines** list stops offering **New**. Its **Subtotal**, **Discount %**, **Tax** and **Shipping & Handling** cannot be changed either, so its **Total Price** stays what was confirmed: the order contract, the payment plan and a framework contract's quota all read it. To change a confirmed order's lines or amount, set its **Status** back to **Draft** on the form and save, change them, and click **Confirm Order** again; the contract and the payment plan then follow the new total as they do for any change while the order is a draft. While it is back in Draft the order does not count towards a framework contract's Order Amount. A completed or cancelled order's lines and amount stay as they are: a difference goes on a new order. The order's other fields, such as the delivery fields, stay editable. Imports and the system's own updates are not held by this rule.

## Delivery

**Fulfillment Status** — *Not Shipped*, *Partially Shipped* or *Shipped* — and **Shipped Date** say how far the order has been delivered. They are typed by hand or filled by an import; nothing in HotCRM changes them, and there is no stock or delivery note behind them.

## Standard list views

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All Orders** *(the landing tab)* | Every order you may see: order number, name, account, status, order date, fulfillment status, total price with a column total, owner. Newest order date first. |
| **Order Board** | The same orders as cards in one column per status. |
| **To Ship** | Confirmed orders that have not fully shipped, oldest order date first. |
