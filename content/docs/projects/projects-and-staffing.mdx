# Projects and Staffing

## Opening a project

A project manager opens a project from **All Projects** with **New**, or from the **Projects** tab of an account, an opportunity or a contract. A project needs a **Project Name** and an **Account**; the **Opportunity** and **Contract** it comes from are offered from that account.

Each project has a **Kind**:

| Kind | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Presales** | Work done to win a deal: a proof of concept, a pilot, a scoping study |
| **Delivery** | Work the customer bought. **Won From** points at the presales project it grew out of, if there was one |
| **Internal** | Work for your own company: practice building, training, tooling |

All three are booked, staffed and costed the same way, so presales effort and internal effort show up as cost just as delivery does. **Billing Type** (Fixed Fee, Time and Materials, Retainer, Non-billable) records how the customer pays.

## Statuses

A project starts as **Draft** and moves forward one step at a time:

| From | To |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | In Approval, Cancelled |
| In Approval | Approved, Rejected, back to Draft, Cancelled |
| Approved | Active, Cancelled |
| Rejected | Draft, Cancelled |
| Active | On Hold, Completed, Cancelled |
| On Hold | Active, Cancelled |
| Completed | Closed, or back to Active |
| Closed, Cancelled | Final |

Any other change is refused with a message that lists these steps. A project is never deleted by its manager: a project that will not go ahead is **Cancelled**, and a finished one is **Closed**.

## The budget

**Approved Budget** is the cost the project may spend. Set it while the project is **Draft** or **Rejected**: from **In Approval** onwards it is read-only, because that is the figure the project was approved on. **Planned Revenue** is what the customer pays for the project, and the margin is measured against it.

The project's [cost and margin fields](/docs/projects#money) fill themselves as hours and expenses are approved. **Budget Used %** passes 100 % when the project has spent more than its approved budget.

## Staffing a project

People are staffed on a project in its **Project Members** tab, or under **Staffing**. Each row names:

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Person** | Who is staffed |
| **Responsibility** | Their part on the project: Project Manager, Project Director, Account Manager, Architect, Consultant, Quality Assurance, Finance Partner or Subcontractor |
| **Rate Card** | The grade they work at. Their hours are costed and billed at its rates |
| **Allocation** | The share of their time the project has, as a percentage |
| **Start Date**, **End Date** | When they are on the project |
| **Billable** | Whether their hours are billed to the customer. A non-billable member's hours still cost, but bill nothing |

Staffing someone does two things on the project:

- **They can read it.** The project's **Team** lists everyone staffed except the Project Manager, and each of them reads the project. Remove the row and they lose that access.
- **The member whose Responsibility is Project Manager becomes the project's Project Manager.** That person reads and edits the project, staffs it and approves its timesheets and expenses, so they are not listed in the Team as well. The field is read-only on the project: change it by changing the staffing.

> **Staff yourself first.** A new project has no Project Manager until someone is staffed with that responsibility. The person who opened it can edit it as its owner, but timesheets and expenses created before a manager is staffed have no approver.

Only people staffed on a project can book hours on it.

## Rate cards

A **Rate Card** is a grade of work with two prices:

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Cost Rate** | What an hour or a day of this grade costs your company |
| **Bill Rate** | What the customer is charged for it |
| **Unit** | Per Hour or Per Day. A day is counted as 8 hours |
| **Seniority** | Junior, Intermediate, Senior, Lead or Principal |
| **Internal** | For internal work. Only an internal card may bill less than it costs |
| **Effective From**, **Effective To** | When the card is in use, for reference: the card on a member's row is the one that counts |

Finance and the PMO keep the rate cards. Everyone reads them, but the cost rate is hidden from team members.

> **Changing a rate card never changes past cost.** Each time entry copies the rates in force when it is written. Hours booked before a change keep the old rates; hours booked after it get the new ones.
