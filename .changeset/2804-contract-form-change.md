**Sales: contract forms, an order contract that follows its order, and Change on an activated contract (#2804).** Every contract now has a **Contract Form**: **Order Contract**, **Framework Contract** or **Other**. It sits beside **Contract Type**, which is unchanged. **Draft Contract** on an order makes an order contract that names the order as its **Source Order**; its value is the order's total, tax included, it is read-only on the form, and while the contract is a draft a change to the order's amount changes the contract's value too. A contract you create by hand is a framework contract (value typed, may carry many orders) or Other (no amount needed, such as an NDA; the value starts at 0); you choose while it is a draft. Once a contract is activated, its **Contract Value**, **End Date** and **Payment Terms** are read-only on the form and change only through the new **Change** button: it asks for a reason and the signed amendment, and saves nothing without both. On a framework contract Change may set a new value; on an order contract it offers no value, only **Update from Order**, which takes the order's current amount. A new end date and new payment terms are optional on both. The contract stays Activated, so renewal reminders and expiry go on as before; Change creates no change order, does not touch the order and does not re-plan payments. History is now kept on the value, the start and end dates, the payment terms and the change reason, so the record's History shows each change before and after, who made it and when. If an order's amount changes after its contract is activated, the contract shows **Differs from the Order Amount**. A direct edit of those three fields on an activated contract, through the form or the API, is now refused and names the field (it used to refuse only an earlier end date). Contracts that already exist take a form on their next save: one drafted by Draft Contract and named by its order is an order contract, one with no value is Other, the rest are framework contracts; values are not changed. After upgrading, an administrator runs `pnpm exec objectstack migrate summary-nulls --apply --yes` (the platform's step that computes **Order Amount** on existing contracts), then `pnpm exec tsx scripts/backfill-contract-form.ts --apply`, which gives every existing contract its form at once and prints the order contracts whose value differs from their order, for the business to check; neither step changes a value. Labels, buttons and messages are in English, Chinese, Spanish and Japanese, and the Contracts manual and in-app help describe the rules.
