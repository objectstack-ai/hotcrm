The **All Leads** list now shows its column headers in the user's language. In Chinese, Spanish and Japanese the nine headers were in English: Name, Company, Email, Status, Score, Source, Need Type, Est. Amount and Owner. The **Account** column on **Open Deals** and **All Opportunities** was also in English. Each header now uses its field's label, which the app translates into every supported language. In English, five lead headers now use the full field name: *Name* is now **Full Name**, *Score* is **Lead Score**, *Source* is **Lead Source**, *Est. Amount* is **Estimated Amount** and *Owner* is **Lead Owner**. The other English headers do not change.
