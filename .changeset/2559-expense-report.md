**Expenses are sent on an expense report, and the report is what is approved and paid back (REQ-0011, #2559).** New object **Expense Report** (`crm_expense_report`, 「报销单」) in the Expenses module: report number, title, submitter, an optional account, **Total** and **To Pay Back** (the employee-paid expenses only), status Draft / Submitted / Approved / Paid / Rejected, the approver, submitted and approved dates, remarks, **Review Notes** (「检查提示」, for an agent or a check to write what it found for the approver), and the payment date and reference. An expense gains **Expense Report**, and its **Status** becomes read-only: it follows its report, back to Draft when the report is recalled or reopened.

**Approval moves FROM each expense TO its report.** FROM `expense_approval` + `expense_approval_on_create` (an expense with no project, to the spender's manager) and `expense_project_approval` + `expense_project_approval_on_create` (an expense on a project, to its Project Manager), each on `crm_expense`; TO `expense_report_approval` (a report with no project, to the submitter's manager, the Finance Controllers when there is none) and `expense_report_project_approval` (a report on a project, to its Project Manager), each on `crm_expense_report`, with a Finance Controller second from a total of 2,000 as before. Nobody approves their own, and nobody sets Approved or Rejected by hand. The *Expenses to Approve* view is replaced by *Reports to Approve*.

**Submit for Reimbursement** (My Expenses selection bar, and the record page for one expense) puts the selected expenses on a report and submits it in the same click; one expense takes no extra step. **One report per project**: each project's expenses go on a report of their own, which names the project, and the rest on one more. The grouping lives in this action, keyed on the expense's Project value, which a record carries only while Project Delivery contributes the field (it now contributes it to the report too, read-only); with Project Delivery disabled every selection makes one report. Any problem (not yours, already on a report, already approved) refuses the whole selection, naming each expense. **Submit** sends a recalled report again; **Back to Draft** reopens a rejected one. **Mark as Paid** lets a Finance Controller or AR Accountant record the payment date and reference on an approved report, from the new *Reports to Pay* view; it records only, moves no money and posts nothing. New views *My Reports*, *Reports to Approve* and *Reports to Pay*; Project Delivery shows *My Expenses* and *Reports to Approve*. Approved expenses count in the project's cost exactly as before, and marking a report paid changes nothing there.

**Deviations from the card, stated:** nobody creates a report by hand (no create grant; Submit for Reimbursement creates it), and finance has no edit-all on reports because Mark as Paid is a script that judges the caller; otherwise the report's grants follow the expense's. The report's approver is copied onto each expense so the approver can open them (sharing rule *Expenses — Approver*, next to the new *Expense Reports — Approver*). A report turned back on a Closed or Cancelled project is deleted and its expenses moved and submitted again, and an expense's project cannot change while it is on any report, even a draft one, because the report goes to the approver of the project it was sent with (`expense_line_lock`); only a draft report can be deleted, and its expenses stay. No seed data: expenses and reports name people, and the seed names none.

**Upgrade note (from 6.5.0 and from #2556), measured on a local database for each:** an approved expense stays approved and keeps counting in its project's cost; it is on no report, so it has no payment record and cannot be submitted again. A draft or rejected expense is submitted for reimbursement like any other. An expense **waiting for approval** at the upgrade cannot be decided afterwards: approving its request answers 409 `RESUME_TARGET_LOST`, because its flow is gone. Recovery: the spender selects it and chooses Submit for Reimbursement, which takes a Submitted or Rejected expense on no report, sets it back to Draft and reports it; the report then opens a new request with the right approver, and approving it approves the expense and moves the project's cost. Recall the old request too (the spender's own recall and an administrator's both answer 200), before or after, so it leaves the approver's Inbox; a recall alone leaves the expense Submitted. The project-path approval and its templates stay in the expense module until objectstack#34616 (hotcrm#2575).
