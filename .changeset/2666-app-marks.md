**Each HotCRM app now has its own icon on the home page and in the app switcher (#2666).** Until now every app showed the same orange HotCRM logo, so the home page's apps row and the app switcher looked like six copies of one app. The **Sales** app keeps the HotCRM logo. **Service**, **Marketing**, **Order to Cash**, **Project Delivery** and **Expenses** now show their own icon on their own colour: Service a headset on blue, Marketing a megaphone on pink, Order to Cash a receipt on green, Project Delivery a briefcase on teal, and Expenses a hand with coins on violet. Inside each of those five apps, buttons and highlights take the app's colour, so Service, Marketing and Order to Cash are no longer orange. The browser tab still shows the HotCRM logo in every app. In the ⌘K command palette, Sales now shows a handshake, so no two apps share an icon there either.
