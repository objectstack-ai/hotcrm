**A task's or an event's Related Opportunity picker offers only the deals of the account the activity names (#2573).** Once **Related Account** is filled in on the New or Edit form, the picker and its **Browse all records** table list that account's deals only, and they narrow again when the account changes. With no account chosen yet, every deal is offered as before. A lost deal is still never offered; a won deal still is, for the delivery and follow-up after the close. The picker narrows the choice only: an import or an API call that links an activity to another account's deal is not refused. The *Activities* page of the docs site and the in-app Sales Manual say so.
