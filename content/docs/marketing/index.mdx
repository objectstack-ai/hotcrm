# Marketing Cloud

The Marketing module lets you **plan campaigns, enroll the right leads and contacts, track who responded, and prove the ROI** to sales and finance.

## What's included

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [**Campaigns**](/docs/marketing/campaigns) | Marketing initiatives — emails, webinars, trade shows, social, content. |
| [**Campaign Members**](/docs/marketing/campaign-members) | The leads and contacts enrolled in each campaign, and how each of them responded. |

## A typical campaign life

```
STATUS    Planning ──► In Progress ──► Completed
          the first two moves are yours; the last is a nightly sweep

MEMBERS   Enroll Members ──► Track responses ──► Convert to opportunities
          Enroll Members is a button you press, not a trigger
```

1. **Planning** — define the campaign, budget, expected revenue, target audience.
2. **Enroll members** — press **Enroll Members** on the campaign and answer its *Enrollment Criteria* screen; everyone eligible in the segment you pick joins as a campaign member at *Sent*. The action is offered while the campaign is *Planning* or *In Progress*, so you can build the audience before you launch and top it up afterwards — see [Campaign enrollment flow](/docs/marketing/campaigns#campaign-enrollment-flow).
3. **In Progress** — launch the campaign. Moving the status is the launch, and that is all the move does: it enrolls nobody.
4. **Track responses** — a member stays at *Sent* until they answer (*Responded*) or their lead converts (*Converted*); someone who asks to stop hearing from you is *Unsubscribed*. There is no ladder between the four, and no open or click step.
5. **Completed** — once its end date has passed, a nightly sweep flips an *In Progress* campaign to *Completed*; the metrics (sent, responded, converted, ROI) go on updating, the same as they did while it ran.
6. **Attribute revenue** — opportunities linked to the campaign roll up into the campaign's actual revenue.

## What the system does for you

- **Automatic conversion tracking** — when an enrolled person's lead is converted, every campaign they are in promotes their member row to *Converted* and recomputes its numbers. That is the one member status the app writes by itself: *Responded* is a rep pressing **Mark Responded**, and *Unsubscribed* is a rep or an opt-out request. Opens, clicks and bounces are not tracked at all.
- **Automatic completion** — an *In Progress* campaign whose end date has passed is marked *Completed* by a sweep that runs at 2 AM daily.
- **Live metrics** — sent, responses, conversions and ROI are recomputed as memberships change and as opportunities are attributed, so an in-flight campaign's numbers are real numbers you can act on today.
- **Budget warnings** — if actual cost exceeds the budgeted cost, you get an over-budget warning.
- **ROI rollup** — the campaign's actual revenue is the sum of related opportunity revenue; ROI = (actual revenue − actual cost) ÷ actual cost.

Enrolling members is deliberately not on that list. Nothing enrolls anybody when a campaign moves to *In Progress*, and no schedule enrolls them later: the audience is answered on a screen, and a scheduled run would arrive with nobody to answer it. Enrolling is a button a marketer presses — see [Campaign enrollment flow](/docs/marketing/campaigns#campaign-enrollment-flow).

## What you can run

There are 8 campaign types out of the box:

- **Email** *(default)* — newsletter blasts, drips, nurture programmes
- **Webinar** — virtual events with registration tracking
- **Trade Show** — in-person booth presence
- **Conference** — speaking slots, sponsorships
- **Direct Mail** — physical letters and packages
- **Social Media** — paid or organic social campaigns
- **Content Marketing** — blog, ebook, whitepaper offers
- **Partner Marketing** — joint campaigns with channel partners

And 5 primary channels: **Digital · Social · Email · Events · Partner**.

## How marketing connects to sales

Every opportunity has a *Campaign* lookup field. Whenever sales closes a deal that came from a campaign, the system attributes the revenue back to the campaign. This means:

- **Marketing can prove ROI** — *"this $500K deal came from our March webinar."*
- **Sales sees campaign context** — when a rep opens a lead enrolled in a campaign, they know the talking point.
- **Forecasts can predict** — campaigns that historically convert at 3% can be modelled into the pipeline.

## Standard reports

- **Lead Inflow (by Month × Source)** — where are the leads coming from?
- **Campaign ROI** *(via campaign list views)* — actual revenue vs. actual cost per campaign.

See [Analytics › Reports](/docs/analytics/reports).

## Where to find things

In the HotCRM app, the **Marketing** group contains two items:

- **Campaigns** — the campaign list (`crm_campaign`).
- **Products** — the product catalog (`crm_product`). This is the catalog's only sidebar entry anywhere in the app, even though the product documentation lives under [Revenue › Products](/docs/revenue/products). Apart from this item and global search, a product record is only reached from a quote or opportunity line item.

The **Marketing** group is **collapsed by default** — unlike Sales, My Work, Activity and Service, it does not open itself when the app loads. Click it open before deciding something isn't there.

Leads enrolled in campaigns are accessed from the campaign detail page or via global search.
