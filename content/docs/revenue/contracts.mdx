# Contracts

A **contract** is the legal agreement between your company and a customer — what they buy, for how long, at what price, on what payment terms. In HotCRM, contracts are the **system of record for active customer relationships**: once activated, they unlock the *Customer* status on the account.

## The 5 statuses

| Status | Color | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ⚪ **Draft** | Gray | Being negotiated, not signed |
| 🔵 **In Approval** | Blue | Sent for internal sign-off |
| 🟢 **Activated** | Green | Signed and in force |
| 🟠 **Expired** | Orange | End date has passed |
| 🔴 **Terminated** | Red | Cancelled early |

Activation is the key transition — it triggers the account to be promoted to *Customer*.

## The 6 contract types

| Type | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscription** | Recurring access — SaaS, software subs |
| **Service** | Time-bounded services — consulting, training |
| **License** | Perpetual software licenses |
| **Partnership** | Reseller, referral, channel partner agreements |
| **NDA** | Non-disclosure (zero-value) |
| **MSA** | Master Service Agreement — umbrella for many SOWs |

## What a contract record stores

A contract record declares its fields in **six groups**. The contract form shows a curated subset of them across its tabs, so read this as the full inventory of what a contract carries rather than a screen-by-screen map:

| Group | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Contract Information** | Contract Number *(auto-numbered `CTR-0001`)*, Contract Type, Contract Document, Description |
| **Parties** | Contract Owner, Account, Primary Contact, Related Opportunity — the contact and opportunity pickers are scoped to the account you pick first |
| **Terms & Dates** | Contract Term (Months), Start Date, End Date, Special Terms |
| **Contract Value** | Contract Value, Billing Frequency, Payment Terms, Billing Address |
| **Status & Approval** | Status, Signed Date, Signed By |
| **Renewal** | Auto Renewal, Renewal Notice (Days) |

Account, Primary Contact, Contract Term, Start Date, End Date, Contract Value and Status are all **required** — a contract cannot be saved without them.

Three field names people go looking for and do not find:

- **Activation Date.** There is none, and no other field records the moment of activation. Activation stamps **Signed Date** instead (see *On contract activation* below), so that is the date any "when did this go live" report has to read.
- **Renewal Terms.** Renewal is exactly the two fields above. Free-text renewal instructions belong in **Special Terms**, which travels with the contract.
- **Account Exec.** Ownership is the single **Contract Owner** lookup; a contract carries no second ownership field.

**Contract Document** is a **link**, not an upload — it holds a URL pointing at wherever the document lives. The executed PDF itself goes on the record as an **attachment**; files are enabled on Contract, which is what makes that possible. There is no separate *Order Form* field.

## Billing frequency

How often the customer is invoiced:

- **One-Time**
- **Monthly**
- **Quarterly**
- **Annual**
- **Custom**

## Payment terms

When the customer pays:

- **Net 15** — within 15 days of invoice
- **Net 30** *(default)* — within 30 days
- **Net 45 / Net 60 / Net 90**
- **Due on Receipt** — immediately
- **Prepaid** — paid upfront

## Renewals

Two fields drive renewals:

- **Auto Renewal** — off unless you turn it on. With it on, the daily renewal sweep opens the renewal deal for you; with it off, the sweep still reminds you but creates no deal.
- **Renewal Notice (Days)** — how many days before the end date the reminder fires. Defaults to 30, and each contract carries its own.

There is no *Renewal Terms* field. Instructions like "renew at 5% uplift unless the customer opts out" go in **Special Terms**, the free-text field on the contract itself.

When a contract enters its renewal window, the system files a follow-up task and emails the contract owner — see *Daily — renewal reminder* below for everything it creates.

## What the system does automatically

### On contract activation
Changing a contract's status **to** *Activated* runs two writes, and only these two:

1. **Signed Date** stamped on the contract — today's date, and only when the contract has none yet. Set Signed Date in the same edit that activates the contract and your date is kept.
2. **Account type → Customer** — every account that is not already a *Customer* is promoted, so this catches a *Partner* or a *Former Customer* just as it catches a *Prospect*.

Nothing is sent and nobody is told. Activation raises no notification for the contract owner or the account owner, and no message goes to the primary contact — there is no outbound customer email here, and no setting behind one. There is no *Customer Since* field on the account either: an account keeps no date of its own for when it became a customer, so cohort reporting has to read the contract's signed date or start date.

Both writes run *after* the save, with failures logged rather than raised, so the contract stays activated even if promoting the account fails. They also hang off an **update** — a contract that arrives already *Activated* (a data import, seed data, an integration that writes the record in one shot) triggers neither, and its account stays a *Prospect*.

### Daily — expiration sweep
Each night at midnight the system finds *Activated* contracts whose end date has passed, and does two things to each — only these two:

1. **Status → Expired**.
2. **Notification to the contract owner** — inbox and email, linking to the contract.

Nobody else is told. There is no second notification: the account owner, the primary contact and the rest of the account team hear nothing when a contract lapses, so if anyone beyond the contract owner needs to know, an admin has to add that. The sweep also reads *Activated* contracts only — one still sitting in *Draft* or *In Approval* past its end date is left alone — and it takes at most 500 contracts per run.

### Daily — renewal reminder
At 8 AM the system finds *Activated* contracts whose end date falls inside that contract's own *Renewal Notice (Days)* window, and for each one, in this order:

1. **Files a renewal task** for the contract owner — *"Renewal due: contract CTR-0042"*, a high-priority follow-up due on the contract's end date, filed against the account.
2. **Notifies the contract owner** — inbox and email. The notification links to the contract record, and that is all it does: there is no one-click action on it, and opening it creates nothing.
3. **Opens a renewal opportunity** — but only when *Auto Renewal* is on. An *Existing Customer - Renewal* deal on the same account, at *Proposal* stage, amount copied from the contract value, close date set to the contract's end date, owner copied from the contract, next step *"Confirm renewal terms with customer"*. It is skipped when that account already has an open renewal deal.

So the renewal deal is never something you click for. With *Auto Renewal* on it is already in the pipeline by the time you read the reminder; with it off, nobody opens one for you and the task is the whole of it.

The task is also what keeps the sweep from repeating itself: the same contract matches every morning of its notice window, and an open *Renewal due* task for it means the day is already handled — no second task, no second notification, no second deal. Complete that task and the next morning's sweep files a fresh one, for as long as the contract is still inside its window.

Two bounds worth knowing: the sweep looks 120 days ahead at most, so a notice window set longer than that only starts firing at 120 days out; and it takes at most 500 contracts per run.

### Manual termination
The owner can change status to *Terminated*. This is a one-way action — terminated contracts can't be reactivated. Audit log records who and when.

## Built-in rules

- **End date must be after start date** — basic sanity check.
- **Contract value must be zero or positive** — NDAs can be $0.
- **Activated contracts cannot be deleted** — change status to Terminated instead.

## Sharing

Contracts are **private**, and no sharing rule widens them. Contract visibility does **not** follow the account:

- **The contract owner** reads and edits their own contracts.
- **Sales Manager** and **System Administrator** read every contract (*View All* on the object); edits stay with the owner except for admins.
- **Everyone else** — including a rep who reached the account through a territory or team sharing rule — sees only the contracts they own, so an account's *Contracts* related list can look empty even when the account itself is fully readable.

If your org wants contracts to follow the account, an admin has to author that, and neither available route actually delivers "follows the account" today: a sharing rule on Contract widens the records it matches for every holder of the object, and a Controlled-by-Parent OWD derives org-wide in this release — the parent link is not consulted per caller. Both are a broad widening, not an account-scoped one — see [Sharing & Security](/docs/administration/sharing-and-security), *Controlled by Parent, in practice*.

## Standard list views

- **My Active Contracts** — `owner_id = me AND status = Activated`.
- **Expiring in 60 Days** — `end_date <= TODAY + 60 AND status = Activated`.
- **Up for Renewal** — within renewal-notice window.
- **Expired This Month** — `status = Expired AND last_modified IN this month`.
- **Pending Activation** — `status IN [Draft, In Approval]`.

## Tips for contract owners

- ✅ **Attach the signed PDF** when activating — auditors will thank you.
- ✅ **Set Renewal Notice (Days) = 90** for enterprise deals — gives you a full quarter to negotiate, and the sweep looks far enough ahead to honour it.
- ✅ **Record what you agreed in Special Terms** — "price-locked for 3 years; 5% uplift on renewal". There is no *Renewal Terms* field, and Special Terms is the note that will still be on the contract when the reminder lands.

## Tips for sales reps

- ✅ When a quote is accepted and you create the contract, the **opportunity link** is pre-filled — keep it; it powers post-sale reporting.
- ✅ Don't sneak edits into Activated contracts — create an amendment instead (a new contract linked to the same account).

## Tips for admins

- Contract activation is an **object hook** (`src/objects/contract.hook.ts`), not a flow — that is where the signed-date stamp and the account promotion live, and it is not in the flow list. The expiration sweep and the renewal reminder *are* scheduled flows. See [Administration › Automation](/docs/administration/automation).
- Contract types and billing frequency picklists are extensible.
