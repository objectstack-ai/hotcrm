**Expenses are their own module, for every employee, with their own Expenses app (REQ-0011, #2556).** New package `app.objectstack.hotcrm.expense` (「费用报销」), switchable per tenant like the other modules. `crm_expense` moves into it from Project Delivery under the same name, so stored expenses keep their rows and their project. An expense gains **Paid By** (Employee by default, or Company), **Merchant**, **Receipt Number** and **Source** (Manual by default, Agent, Import); none is required, and no currency field is added. Every internal permission set records, reads and edits its own expenses and sees the Expenses app; finance (Finance Controller, AR Accountant) and the sales manager and leadership read every expense. **An expense with no project is approved by the spender's manager** (the Manager on their user record; the Finance Controllers when there is none), then by a Finance Controller from 2,000 as before. **An expense on a project is approved by its Project Manager**, then finance from 2,000, and approved expenses count in the project's cost exactly as before. The approver is now named when the expense is submitted, not when it is created. Project Delivery depends on the expense module and contributes the **Project** field, whose picker no longer offers a Closed or Cancelled project; project staff keep *My Expenses* and *Expenses to Approve* in the Project Delivery app. An expense submitted on a Closed, Cancelled or locked project now goes back to Draft and its spender gets an inbox notice naming the project, instead of the save being refused. With Project Delivery disabled the Project field leaves the expense and every expense goes to the manager; with the expense module disabled, projects and timesheets work and the expense entries leave the Project Delivery app. **Upgrade note:** an expense approval still pending when you upgrade cannot be decided afterwards (the approval belonged to Project Delivery's old flow): decide pending expense approvals before upgrading, or afterwards have an administrator recall the request from the approvals Inbox, after which the spender sets the expense back to Draft and submits it again. Expenses saved before the upgrade keep every value and their project; their new fields are empty. For now the project-path approval is registered by the expense module rather than Project Delivery, for a platform lint gap (objectstack#34616); it moves back on hotcrm#2575.
