Sales reps, service agents and marketing users now see the follow-on updates their own saves trigger: a rep's won deal or converted lead updates its campaign, a rep's win promotes the account to customer, an agent's resolved case refreshes the account's last activity date, an agent's own escalation hands the case to a service manager, a marketing user's contact unsubscribe opts the contact out, a marketing user's enrollment no longer drops Private won deals from the campaign's numbers, and a withdrawn knowledge-article vote stops being counted.
