# Revenue Cloud

The Revenue module covers everything that happens **after sales says "we won"**: managing the product catalog and prices, governing contracts and renewals, and approving high-value discounts.

## What's included

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [**Products**](/docs/revenue/products) | The catalog of what you sell — names, codes, list prices, billing types. |
| [**Contracts**](/docs/revenue/contracts) | Signed agreements with customers — terms, value, renewals. |
| [**Approvals**](/docs/revenue/approvals) | Multi-step approval for high-value discounts. |
| [**Billing Hand-off**](/docs/revenue/billing-handoff) | Where CRM scope ends — the two outbound events that hand a won deal and an activated contract to billing. |

## The revenue lifecycle

```
Product (catalog)
   │
   ▼
Quote with line items
   │ (accepted)
   ▼
Contract (Activated)
   │
   ▼ (renewal_notice_days before end_date)
Renewal task ──► Renewal opportunity
```

1. **Set up the catalog** — Products with codes, list prices, billing types.
2. **Build quotes** with line items from the catalog (see [Quotes](/docs/sales/quotes)).
3. **On acceptance** the system creates a contract with the quote's terms.
4. **Contract is activated** — the account is promoted to *Customer*.
5. **Before the contract ends**, the system schedules a renewal task and a renewal reminder.
6. **Renewal opportunity** can be created when the customer wants to extend.

## What the system does for you

- **Auto-promote account** to *Customer* the moment a contract is activated.
- **Auto-stamp Signed Date** on the contract at that same activation — today's date, and only when the contract carries none yet. No date is stamped on the *account*: there is no **Customer Since** field, and no field of any name records when an account became a customer, so a cohort report has to read the contract's **Signed Date** or **Start Date**.
- **Daily expiration sweep** — at midnight, contracts past their end date are marked *Expired* and the owner is notified.
- **Daily renewal reminder** — when a contract is within its own notice window (**Renewal Notice (Days)**, 30 unless you change it), the **contract owner** gets a renewal task and a reminder in their inbox and by email. Nobody else is told — not the account owner, not the account's renewal owner.
- **Discount approval** — opportunities over $500K auto-route for manager + director sign-off.

## Where to find things

There is no **Products** group in the HotCRM app. The catalog's only sidebar entry is **Products**, and it sits under the **Marketing** group — see [Marketing](/docs/marketing/index). Apart from that item and global search, a product record is reached only from a quote or an opportunity line item.

**Contracts** is under the **Sales** group, alongside Leads, Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities, Pipeline and Quotes, because contracts belong to the sales process. That entry is the app's only sidebar route to a contract.

The **Approvals** group holds exactly one item:

- **Inbox** — opens the platform's **approval centre**, where the approvals waiting on you are approved or rejected. **Inbox** is the label you see in the sidebar; nothing in the app is called *Approval Requests*.

Two names this page used to list are not in that group:

- **Action History** — no sidebar item carries that name. The audit trail is real data — the approvals plugin stores it as `sys_approval_action` — but no navigation entry in this app opens it.
- **Processes** — deliberately removed, not an oversight: no `sys_approval_process` object exists in any installed plugin, so the item's *requires object* guard hid it on every install, forever. It stays out until such an object ships.

**Marketing** and **Approvals** are both **collapsed by default** — unlike Sales, My Work, Activity and Service, they do not open when the app loads. Click a group open before deciding something isn't there.
