# Payroll & Benefits

Accurate, timely compensation is a cornerstone of employee trust. HotCRM's Payroll & Benefits module handles the full spectrum of compensation management — from salary calculations and tax withholding to time-off tracking and benefits administration — ensuring your workforce is paid correctly and on time, every time.

## Payroll Processing

### Salary Management

Manage the complete compensation lifecycle for every employee.

- **Payroll Periods**: Support for monthly, bi-weekly, and weekly pay cycles
- **Gross Salary Calculation**: Base salary plus overtime, bonuses, commissions, and allowances
- **Deductions**: Automatic calculation of taxes, insurance premiums, retirement contributions, and other withholdings
- **Net Pay**: Transparent breakdown from gross to net with line-item detail
- **Payment Methods**: Support for bank transfer, check, cash, and direct deposit
- **Multi-Currency**: Handle payroll in multiple currencies for global teams

### Payroll Workflow

A structured process ensures accuracy and compliance.

1. **Preparation**: System gathers attendance, time-off, and overtime data for the pay period
2. **Calculation**: Automatic computation of gross pay, deductions, and net pay
3. **Review**: Payroll manager reviews and validates calculations before processing
4. **Approval**: Finance or HR director approves the payroll run
5. **Distribution**: Payments are processed and pay stubs are generated
6. **Reconciliation**: Post-payroll audit to verify accuracy

### Tax & Compliance

Stay compliant with local, state, and federal regulations.

- **Tax Withholding**: Automatic calculation based on employee tax brackets and filing status
- **Year-End Reporting**: Generate W-2s, 1099s, and other tax documents
- **Regulatory Updates**: System accommodates changing tax rates and labor regulations
- **Audit Trail**: Complete history of every payroll calculation for compliance audits

## Time & Attendance

### Attendance Tracking

Monitor employee work hours with precision.

- **Clock In/Out**: Daily attendance tracking with check-in and check-out timestamps
- **Status Tracking**: Present, Absent, Late, On Leave — with automatic flagging of anomalies
- **Location Verification**: GPS-based check-in for remote and field employees
- **Overtime Detection**: Automatic identification of hours exceeding standard work schedules
- **Shift Management**: Support for multiple shifts and rotating schedules

### Time Off Management

Streamline leave requests and approvals.

- **Leave Types**: Vacation, Sick Leave, Personal Days, Maternity/Paternity Leave, Bereavement, and custom categories
- **Request Workflow**: Employee submits → Manager reviews → HR confirms → Calendar updated
- **Balance Tracking**: Real-time view of accrued, used, and remaining leave balances
- **Approval Chains**: Multi-level approval for extended leaves
- **Blackout Dates**: Prevent leave requests during critical business periods
- **Team Calendar**: Visual calendar showing team availability to prevent scheduling conflicts

### Holiday Management

Configure company and regional holiday calendars.

- **Holiday Calendars**: Create regional calendars with public holidays and company-specific dates
- **Paid vs. Unpaid**: Distinguish between paid holidays and unpaid closures
- **Calendar Assignment**: Assign different holiday calendars to employees by region or department
- **SLA Integration**: Holiday calendars automatically adjust SLA timers in Service Cloud

## Benefits Administration

### Health & Insurance

Manage employee benefits enrollment and tracking.

- **Plan Options**: Health, dental, vision, life insurance, and disability coverage
- **Open Enrollment**: Annual enrollment periods with plan comparison tools
- **Dependent Management**: Add spouses, children, and dependents to benefit plans
- **Premium Tracking**: Monitor employer and employee contribution amounts

### Retirement & Savings

Help employees plan for the future.

- **401(k) / Pension**: Retirement plan enrollment and contribution management
- **Employer Matching**: Configure and track employer matching contributions
- **Vesting Schedules**: Track vesting progress for equity and retirement benefits

### Perks & Allowances

Track additional employee benefits beyond standard compensation.

- **Equipment Allowances**: Laptop, phone, and home office stipends
- **Education Reimbursement**: Tuition assistance and professional development budgets
- **Wellness Programs**: Gym memberships, mental health resources, and wellness incentives
- **Transportation**: Commuting subsidies and parking benefits

## Compensation Analytics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total Compensation Cost** | Sum of all salaries, bonuses, benefits, and employer taxes |
| **Cost per Employee** | Average total compensation per headcount |
| **Overtime Percentage** | Overtime hours as a proportion of total hours worked |
| **Leave Utilization** | Percentage of available PTO days actually used |
| **Benefits Enrollment Rate** | Percentage of eligible employees enrolled in benefit plans |
| **Payroll Accuracy** | Percentage of payroll runs requiring zero corrections |

## Integration Points

- **Performance Management**: Performance ratings feed directly into merit increase and bonus calculations
- **Recruitment**: Offer letters pull salary ranges and benefits packages from compensation tables
- **Finance Cloud**: Payroll data syncs with accounting for expense recognition and budgeting
- **Attendance & Scheduling**: Time data flows automatically into payroll calculations

## Best Practices

1. **Automate Calculations**: Let the system handle gross-to-net calculations — manual adjustments invite errors
2. **Review Before Processing**: Always have a second pair of eyes review payroll before distribution
3. **Encourage Self-Service**: Empower employees to view pay stubs, update tax withholding, and submit time-off requests themselves
4. **Plan for Compliance**: Stay ahead of regulatory changes by reviewing tax tables and labor laws quarterly
5. **Track Everything**: Use the audit trail for every payroll change — it's your best defense during audits
