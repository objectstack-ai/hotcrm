# Orders

An **order** records what a customer bought this time: the products or services, how many, and at what price. It is the record a deal ends in. A **contract** records what the two sides agreed, and is optional: an order can sit under a framework contract, and most orders have none.

| | Order | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Records | What was bought this time | What the two sides agreed |
| Lines | Yes; the subtotal is the sum of the lines | No lines |
| Dates | Order date, shipped date | Start, end, term, renewal |
| When | A sale | A subscription, project services, a framework agreement, an NDA |

An order belongs to at most one contract; a contract may hold many orders, or none.

## Where to find orders

- **Orders** in the **Sales** group of the Sales app.
- The **Orders** list on an account's page, and on the page of a contract an order sits under.
- **Orders, Contracts and Accounts** in the Order to Cash app, where finance reads them.

Orders are shown to the people who work with quotes: sales reps, sales managers, sales leadership and administrators, plus finance (Accounts Receivable and the Finance Controller), who read them. Service and marketing staff do not see them.

## The statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| ⚪ **Draft** | Being put together *(default for new orders)* |
| 🔵 **Confirmed** | Agreed with the customer; its lines are locked |
| 🟢 **Completed** | Delivered and done |
| ⚫ **Cancelled** | Will not go ahead |

An order moves **Draft → Confirmed → Completed**. **Confirmed** can go back to **Draft**, and **Draft** or **Confirmed** can be **Cancelled**. **Completed** and **Cancelled** are final; any other status change is refused when you save.

## What an order record stores

| Section | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Order Information** | Order number, name, owner, account, contact, opportunity, status, order date, contract, customer PO number, description |
| **Pricing** | Subtotal, discount %, discount amount, tax, shipping & handling, total price, payment terms |
| **Delivery** | Fulfillment status, shipped date |
| **Addresses** | Billing address, shipping address |
| **System** | External reference |

The **Customer PO Number** is the number on the customer's own purchase order. The **External Reference** is the order's number in another system, such as the order system it came from; no two orders share one.

## Order lines

Each line carries a product, its title, a description, the quantity, the list price, the sales price, a line discount, and the line's subtotal and total, the same as a quote line.

- **Choose a product** and the line takes the product's name as its title and the product's list price, and the sales price starts at the list price until you type another.
- **No product?** Type the line's **Product Title** and **Sales Price**. This is how you add something the catalogue does not sell, and how lines are entered when the Quotes and Products module is switched off (its product picker is then empty).
- A line's list price comes from the product itself, not from a price book.

## How the totals are calculated

```
Subtotal         = Σ line (quantity × sales price × (1 − line discount %))
Discount Amount  = Subtotal × order discount %
Total Price      = Subtotal − Discount Amount + Tax + Shipping & Handling
```

The subtotal is recalculated on every line you add, change or remove, and the discount amount and total price follow every change to the subtotal, the discount, the tax or the shipping. An order with no lines keeps the subtotal you type.

## A confirmed order's lines are locked

Once an order leaves **Draft**, its lines cannot be added, changed or removed, and the **Order Lines** list stops offering **New**. To change a confirmed order's lines, move it back to **Draft**, change them, and confirm it again. A completed or cancelled order's lines stay as they are. The order's other fields, such as the delivery fields, stay editable.

## Delivery

**Fulfillment Status** — *Not Shipped*, *Partially Shipped* or *Shipped* — and **Shipped Date** say how far the order has been delivered. They are typed by hand or filled by an import; nothing in HotCRM changes them, and there is no stock or delivery note behind them.

## Standard list views

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All Orders** *(the landing tab)* | Every order you may see: order number, name, account, status, order date, fulfillment status, total price with a column total, owner. Newest order date first. |
| **Order Board** | The same orders as cards in one column per status. |
| **To Ship** | Confirmed orders that have not fully shipped, oldest order date first. |
