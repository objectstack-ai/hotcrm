**Order to Cash: plan steps, invoices and payments belong to the account; the contract becomes an optional source (REQ-0010, #2557).** Billing milestones, invoices and payments now hang off the **account** (their master) instead of the contract. So an invoice for an order, one invoice for several orders, and an advance payment that matches no invoice yet can be recorded against the account alone. A billing milestone still names the contract it plans.

- **Required field, FROM → TO:** on `crm_billing_milestone`, `crm_invoice` and `crm_payment`, the required master changes from **Contract** (`crm_contract`, master-detail) to **Account** (`crm_account`, master-detail; was a read-only stamped lookup). **Contract** becomes an optional lookup on all three; a billing milestone without a contract is refused (`billing_milestone_contract`).
- On the form, choosing a contract fills in the account. On every save the account is taken from the contract, and a contract of another account is refused, naming the contract. An invoice that names a milestone but no contract, or a payment that names an invoice or a milestone but no contract, takes its contract from it.
- A payment against another account's invoice is refused (was: another contract's).
- An invoice with no contract has no payment terms: its due date is typed.
- The contract page counts the records that name the contract; the account page counts all of the account's records, those with no contract included. On existing data both read the same as before.
- Deleting a contract still deletes its plan steps and is still refused while invoices or payments name it. Deleting an account deletes its plan steps and is refused while it has invoices or payments.
- **Invoice Import** and **Payment Import** gain an **Account** column (the account name); **Contract** is optional. The templates carry the new column.
- Who reads and writes plan steps, invoices and payments is unchanged for sales reps, sales managers and finance.
- People who read accounts but not contracts (the CRM User default set, Marketing User, Service Agent, Project Member) still read no invoice and no payment: those four sets no longer hold read on the two objects, which would now open every row of the accounts they read (ruled 2026-11-17).
- **Who reads the account's Invoiced, Received and Receivable Balance, FROM → TO** (ruling C, #2557): FROM everyone who reads the account, TO a business role. **CRM User**, the default set every member holds, now hides the three; a set that opens them with its own field rows (Sales Rep, Sales Leadership, AR Accountant, Marketing User, Service Agent, Service Director) or with View All / Modify All on accounts (Sales Manager, Finance Controller, Project Manager, System Administrator, Tenant Administrator) reads them as before. A member who holds CRM User and nothing else, and a consultant (Project Member), read the account without them. The contract's totals are unchanged.
- Demo data: Wayne Enterprises has a 10,000 deposit recorded against the account alone.

**Upgrading an existing environment (FROM 6.5.0 → TO this version).** Measured on a local SQLite database created by 6.5.0 (`v6.5.0`, @objectstack 18.6.0) with Order to Cash rows on CTR-0002 / Wayne Enterprises, then booted on this version:

1. **Your own permission sets and the account's totals.** A permission set your organization authored that reads accounts, but neither names `crm_account.invoiced_amount`, `received_amount` and `receivable_balance` readable nor holds View All on accounts, loses the three totals after the upgrade: its holders also hold CRM User, which now hides them. Measured: a member holding such a set read all three on 6.5.0 and none after the upgrade, while still reading the account. To keep them, add `{ readable: true }` rows for the three fields to that set.
2. **Contract no longer required: one administrator step on an environment you run yourself.** No data moves: every plan step, invoice and payment keeps its account and its contract, and every contract and account total reads the same (measured). The database still holds the three contract columns as NOT NULL, because a boot never relaxes a column, so until the step a record with no contract is refused with "Contract is required" and the demo deposit is reported as one seed error on boot. The step: run `os migrate apply` once and confirm the three `relax_not_null` changes it lists (`crm_billing_milestone.crm_contract`, `crm_invoice.crm_contract`, `crm_payment.crm_contract`); skip the three `tighten_not_null` changes on the account columns it marks destructive, as nothing needs them. Measured: applied 3, skipped 3; after a restart an invoice and a payment saved with the account alone, the demo deposit seeded, and only the account those records name moved. The admin manual's Go-Live Checklist carries the same note.
3. **Hosted environments: no step.** A hosted tenant cannot run `os migrate apply`, and does not need to. This version declares the three contract columns `storage: { notNull: false }`, and a managed install or upgrade relaxes the NOT NULL columns the new package version declares nullable (objectstack#34377 and #33724: cloud PR #4753 and framework PR #34408, both in the hosted pin on @objectstack 18.6.0). Measured locally with the platform's own `declaredNullableColumns` over this version's Order to Cash package: it names all three contract columns. The hosted re-save itself, an invoice or payment saved with no contract after a hosted upgrade, has not been read yet; that reading is objectstack#35262's.
