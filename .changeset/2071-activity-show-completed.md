Calls and meetings you log on a case, an opportunity or an account now appear on that record. **Log a Call** and **Log a Meeting** saved the interaction, but the record page never listed it: on a case or an opportunity it was missing from the **Activity** tab, and on an account, whose activity shows in the **Discussion** feed below the tabs, it was missing there. These feeds now list the logged calls and meetings. The case's *resolved* and *closed* entries and the opportunity's *won* and *lost* entries were hidden in the same way, and now appear too. Leads already showed their logged calls and are unchanged. Contacts do not change yet: a contact uses the platform's standard record page, which still hides logged calls.
