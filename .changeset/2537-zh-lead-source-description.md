**The Chinese description of the Sales Performance "Lead Source" donut names the right measure (#2537).** The tile sums the amount of every opportunity that is not Closed Lost, grouped by lead source, but its zh-CN subtitle read 按来源统计的线索数量 ("number of leads by source"), so a reader took currency for a lead count. It now reads 按线索来源统计的管道金额, in line with the English, Spanish and Japanese descriptions ("Where our pipeline is coming from"). The title 线索来源分布 is unchanged.
