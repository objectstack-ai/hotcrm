# Approvals

HotCRM ships with one built-in approval process: the **Opportunity Discount Approval**. It enforces a "two pairs of eyes" rule on big-money discounts so no rep can give away margin without manager and director sign-off.

## What triggers an approval

The Opportunity Discount Approval is triggered when:

- An **opportunity** is submitted for approval, AND
- The **opportunity amount is greater than $500,000**

Below the threshold, no approval is needed — the rep can move the opportunity directly to *Closed Won*.

## The 2-step process

| Step | Approver | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Sales Manager** | The submitter's manager (from the role hierarchy) | First line of defense — sanity-check the deal |
| **2. Sales Director** | The sales director role | Final sign-off — protect company margin |

Both approvals must be granted in order. Rejection at either step sends the request back.

## What submitters see

When a sales rep clicks **Submit for Approval** on a qualifying opportunity:

1. They're prompted for a **comment** explaining the discount rationale.
2. The opportunity is **locked** — no edits to amount, stage, or close date until the process resolves.
3. The status indicator shows **In Approval**.
4. The first approver gets an email and an in-app notification.

## What approvers see

Each approver receives:

- An **email** with the opportunity summary, amount, account, stage, and the submitter's comment.
- An **in-app task** in their *Approval Inbox*.
- A one-click **Approve** / **Reject** with a comment box.

Approvers can also delegate during PTO (configured in their profile).

## First-response behavior

The process uses **first-response** behavior — the first approver to respond at each step (in case of delegates or multiple eligible approvers) decides the outcome. No "wait for all" voting.

## What happens on each outcome

### ✅ Approved (both steps)
1. Opportunity is **unlocked**.
2. Status returns to its previous stage (typically *Negotiation* or *Proposal*).
3. A note is added to the activity timeline: *"Discount approved by [Director] on [date]"*.
4. The rep can now move the opportunity to *Closed Won*.

### ❌ Rejected (at any step)
- The opportunity moves **back to the previous step** in the process.
- If rejected at Step 1, the submitter is notified and the opportunity is unlocked for revision.
- If rejected at Step 2, it goes back to Step 1 for the manager to re-review (after the submitter revises the deal).
- The rejection comment is captured in the audit log.

### ⏪ Recalled
The submitter can recall the request as long as no one has acted on it. The opportunity is unlocked and returns to its previous stage.

## Record locking

While in approval, these fields on the opportunity are **read-only for everyone except the system**:

- Amount
- Stage
- Close Date
- Probability

The rest of the record (description, next steps, notes) remains editable.

## The approval audit trail

Every action is logged in the opportunity's *Approval History* section:

| Date | Step | Actor | Action | Comment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-03-12 09:14 | Submit | Alex Chen | Submitted | "5% discount to close before Q1" |
| 2024-03-12 11:30 | 1 | Sam Lee | Approved | "OK given strategic logo" |
| 2024-03-12 14:02 | 2 | Pat Wong | Approved | "Approved — book it" |

This history is permanent and surfaces in deal-review reports.

## Where to find pending approvals

In the Enterprise CRM app, the **Approvals** group:

- **Approval Requests** — everything pending, with views *Pending My Approval*, *Submitted by Me*, *Recently Approved*.
- **Action History** — the full audit trail across all approvals.
- **Processes** — admin view of the approval rules and steps.

## Tips for sales reps

- ✅ **Submit early** — don't wait until the customer is signing. Most approvals come back same-day.
- ✅ **Write a strong rationale** in the comment — "strategic logo", "competitive displacement", "expansion follow-on". Approvers reject vague justifications.
- ✅ **Don't keep editing the opportunity after submission** — the record is locked. Recall, edit, resubmit.

## Tips for managers

- ✅ **Respond within 24 hours** — sales velocity matters; a stuck approval kills deal momentum.
- ✅ **Use the comment field** even on approvals — *"Approved; please attach signed NDA before booking"*.
- ✅ **Reject with specifics** — *"Need exec sponsor letter from customer"* is far more useful than *"Not yet"*.

## Tips for admins

- The threshold ($500K), the steps (manager → director), and the lock fields can all be customized.
- You can add **additional approval processes** for different objects — contract approval, expense approval, etc.
- The approval process definition lives alongside other automation in the codebase; see [Administration › Automation](/docs/administration/automation) for how to configure new ones.
- Delegated approvers are set in user profile preferences. See [Administration › Profiles](/docs/administration/profiles).
