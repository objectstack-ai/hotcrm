Submissions from the public Web-to-Lead (Contact Us) and Web-to-Case (Support) forms are now routed like every other incoming lead and case: a web lead goes to the sales rep with the fewest open leads, and its new-lead alert reaches that rep; a web case goes to the service agent with the fewest open cases, instead of waiting in Unassigned — triage while agents are on the rota. A web enquiry from an address already in the CRM is now flagged as a suspected duplicate, and a sales rep's new lead is now flagged when it repeats a colleague's contact or lead the rep cannot see. The visitor who fills in a public form is still told only that the submission was received — never who it went to, or whether the address was already known.
