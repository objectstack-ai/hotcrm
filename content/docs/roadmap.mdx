# HotCRM Roadmap

HotCRM has completed **10 development phases**, establishing a full-featured, production-ready **6-Cloud CRM** with **94 business objects**, AI agent workflows, enterprise security, full UI metadata, Salesforce feature parity (~92%), and comprehensive developer tooling. Our roadmap now focuses on **deep metadata adoption**, **ecosystem growth**, **vertical solutions**, and **advanced AI** for 2026–2027+.

> **Protocol Version**: @objectstack/spec v3.0.0 | **Tests**: 2,200+ passing (132+ files) | **Spec Schema Adoption**: 55 / ~80 (69%) | **Last Updated**: June 2026
