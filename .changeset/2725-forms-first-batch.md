**The product and event attendee dialogs now use the standard layout, and six unused lead forms are gone.**

- **Products.** The New and Edit dialogs now show the same three sections as the product record page: Product Information, Pricing and Resources. Before, they showed Product Info, Pricing and Media. SKU, Active and Product Manager now sit under Product Information. Description moves out of Media into Product Information. The image and datasheet are in Resources, which starts collapsed. Every field you could enter before is still there. The automatic product code now appears only on the record page.
- **Event attendees.** The dialog keeps its Attendee and Invitation sections with the same fields. It is now drawn from the attendee's field groups, not from a hand-written copy of them.
- **Leads.** Six named lead forms are deleted: a tabbed form, a conversion wizard and four layout examples. Nothing in the app opened them. Leads are still converted with *Convert Lead*. The lead New and Edit dialogs and the public *Contact Us* form are unchanged.
