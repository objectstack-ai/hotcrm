# Revenue Cloud

The Revenue module covers everything that happens **after sales says "we won"**: managing the product catalog and prices, governing contracts and renewals, and approving high-value discounts.

## What's included

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [**Products**](/docs/revenue/products) | The catalog of what you sell — names, codes, list prices, billing types. |
| [**Contracts**](/docs/revenue/contracts) | Signed agreements with customers — terms, value, renewals. |
| [**Approvals**](/docs/revenue/approvals) | Multi-step approval for high-value discounts. |

## The revenue lifecycle

```
Product (catalog)
   │
   ▼
Quote with line items
   │ (accepted)
   ▼
Contract (Activated)
   │
   ▼ (renewal_notice_days before end_date)
Renewal task ──► Renewal opportunity
```

1. **Set up the catalog** — Products with codes, list prices, billing types.
2. **Build quotes** with line items from the catalog (see [Quotes](/docs/sales/quotes)).
3. **On acceptance** the system creates a contract with the quote's terms.
4. **Contract is activated** — the account is promoted to *Customer*.
5. **Before the contract ends**, the system schedules a renewal task and a renewal reminder.
6. **Renewal opportunity** can be created when the customer wants to extend.

## What the system does for you

- **Auto-promote account** to *Customer* the moment a contract is activated.
- **Auto-stamp Customer Since** date on the first activation.
- **Daily expiration sweep** — at midnight, contracts past their end date are marked *Expired* and the owner is notified.
- **Daily renewal reminder** — when a contract is within its renewal-notice window (e.g., 60 days before end), the owner and the account owner get a reminder email.
- **Discount approval** — opportunities over $500K auto-route for manager + director sign-off.

## Where to find things

In the Enterprise CRM app, the **Products** group contains:

- **Products** — the catalog

And the **Sales** group contains **Contracts** (because they belong to the sales process).

Approvals live in the **Approvals** group:

- **Approval Requests** — what's pending
- **Action History** — who approved what, when
- **Processes** — admin view of the approval rules
